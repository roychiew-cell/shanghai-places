Late-November weather

Read on 23 Nov 2026 for central Shanghai, when these days were 1 to 7 days ahead, so confidence is moderate; forecasts sharpen inside a week.

The short answer

A cold start, then warmer and dry

Tue 24 and Wed 25 Nov are cold, with highs near 13 and 12 °C. Wednesday is the coldest day: about 4 °C at its lowest. The models show temperatures falling through Tuesday evening, then a quick warm-up to 18 °C on Thursday and about 20 °C on Friday. The rest of 24 to 30 Nov stays mostly in the mid to high teens by day, with little rain expected.

Each forecast system is an ensemble: many runs of one weather model with slightly different starting conditions.

What stands out

  • Wednesday is about 4 °C colder than the 18 Nov reading: the middle forecast fell from 8 to 4 °C for the low and from 16 to 12 °C for the high. Tuesday also cooled, from 10 to 6 °C for the low and 15 to 13 °C for the high.
  • The cold spell is short. The hourly models show Tuesday falling from about 12 °C in early afternoon to about 6 °C late in the evening. Thursday's high is about 18 °C and Friday's about 20 °C.
  • Rain looks unlikely throughout 24 to 30 Nov: the chance of at least 1 mm is 0 to 2% each day. The middle and 9 in 10 ensemble rain totals are 0.0 mm on every day shown.
  • Night lows remain uncertain. For Wednesday, the five ensemble medians range from about -1 to 7 °C; the middle 80% of the combined forecast puts the low at about -1 to 7 °C. The daytime high has a narrower 11 to 14 °C range.
  • Tuesday is the windiest of the first days: the middle forecast for the day's strongest wind is 26 km/h, with nine tenths of the combined forecast at or below about 34 km/h. Saturday's figures are about 22 and 32 km/h.

What to wear

  • Bring a proper warm coat and layers for Tuesday evening and Wednesday. A scarf or thermal layer will help if the colder night forecast comes through.
  • A light jacket should suit Thursday to Sunday afternoons; keep a layer for cooler mornings and evenings.
  • Monday looks warmer, near 20 °C by day, so one lighter layer should do for the afternoon. Rain gear is a small precaution, with no day above a 2% chance of at least 1 mm.

How this was made

  • Forecasts: Open-Meteo, read on 23 Nov 2026. Nine global models (ECMWF IFS and AIFS, US GFS, German ICON, Canadian GEM, Japan JMA, China CMA GRAPES, UK Met Office, French ARPEGE) and five ensembles for central Shanghai.
  • History: ERA5 reanalysis for 2006 to 2025 within two days of each date, and the Pudong (ZSPD) and Hongqiao (ZSSS) airport thermometers over the same years (Iowa Environmental Mesonet archive of airport reports).
  • The table's low and high ranges are the rounded middle 80% of the combined forecast, not a guarantee. Night lows vary more between models than daytime highs, especially around Wednesday. The number of daily model forecasts thins from 27 Nov, although all five ensembles still report for each day shown.
  • The airport thermometers used for the historical comparison are about 13 km (Hongqiao) and 33 km (Pudong) from central Shanghai. Temperatures in the city centre may differ, especially at night.
  • Nothing here is an official forecast: China's national forecast is not included.
