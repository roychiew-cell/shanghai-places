Adult clothing

This section lists five places to shop for adult clothing in Shanghai, with the emphasis on menswear. They are ranked by how well each was checked and how useful it is for menswear, not by prestige. If you want a made-to-measure suit, start with South Bund, because it needs a fitting partway through; the other four are for ready-to-wear.

How the five fit together

South Bund is where to be measured for a suit; Plaza 66 and HKRI Taikoo Hui make one trip; Bicester Village is a separate half-day; Wukang and Anfu Roads are a walk past small designer shops. Less certain here: no Dianping or Amap ratings and no Xiaohongshu posts were read for any place, only one dated rating (Tripadvisor, South Bund) is shown, prices are missing for several places, and no Black Friday (27 Nov 2026) programme was found.

Considered and left out (12)

These were looked at and left out. Each line says why.

  • Shanghai Florentia Village (上海佛罗伦萨小镇), Pudong near the airportIt is a fashion-house outlet (Winshang, 5 Aug 2026 names Gucci, Prada, Burberry, Celine, Fendi and Balenciaga). Zegna lists a Shanghai Florentia Village outlet; current tailoring stock and Canali's presence are unconfirmed. Its 2026 evidence is mostly from the operator, and Phase 3 was still unopened on 5 Aug 2026; a September report says it is planned for the end of 2026. It is a possible stop on the way to Pudong airport: about a 10-minute drive from the airport, with free luggage storage and an instant tax refund of up to 9 percent (Winshang, 6 Jul 2026), open 10:00 to 21:00 on Mon to Thu (Shanghai government listing, updated 26 Dec 2025); take Line 2 to Yuandong Avenue, then the free shuttle. Address: 上海市浦东新区卓耀路58弄1-365号.
  • iapm (环贸iapm), 999 Middle Huaihai RoadStill open (the Shanghai government's Feb 2026 first-store list shows a new store there, and listings give late hours), but Gucci closed its 720 sqm flagship there on 17 Mar 2026 (Jing Daily, 12 Mar 2026), and no angle was found where it beats the picks. Fine as a late-opening fallback only.
  • Qipu Road Clothing Market (七浦路服装批发市场)A lead only. SmartShanghai (updated 26 Jun 2026) lists it as open daily 8:00 to 18:00, but warns of fakes, touts and poor-quality clothes, and a 2023 Pengpai report found the upper floors of a Qipu market largely shut. It is wholesale with bargaining and probably cash, a poor fit for a visitor who needs card or phone-wallet payment.
  • HAI550 (淮海中路550号)Operating (Shanghai government, early 2026) but a sustainability-themed hub of small brands. About half its tenants change within a year (Shanghai government), and the only menswear item found is Youngor's own gallery-style shop (launch coverage Aug 2024). A short browse next to TX Huaihai, not a pick.
  • Xintiandi itself (新天地), as a clothing destinationIt is described as upscale local and international designer retail, but no 2026 clothing-specific evidence was found for it, so it is left out.
  • Greenland Bund Global Custom Centre (绿地外滩全球定制中心, Dongjiadu, South Bund)Announced as open by the Shanghai government on 10 Jun 2026; Caiwen (29 Sep 2026) reported it as opening on 29 Sep. About 10,000 sqm in Greenland Bund Phases II and III, with qipao, suits and wedding wear, a haute-couture centre and a departure tax-refund function. No street address was found, and it is too new to judge, so it is not a substitute for the main South Bund market. Watch only.
  • Shanghai Tang Xintiandi store (上海滩 新天地)Only a 2021 listing was seen. It could not be confirmed that the store is open or still offers made-to-measure Chinese-style menswear in 2026.
  • Meilongzhen Plaza (梅龙镇广场), 1038 Nanjing West RoadWinshang (12 Aug 2026) mentions a planned renovation by Hang Lung with no timeline. One unconfirmed report says it is closed for rebuild with retail reopening in 2027. Do not plan on it.
  • Qingpu Bailian Outlets Phase 2 (青浦百联奥莱二期)Winshang's monthly review (10 Oct 2026) lists its opening in September 2026. It is far west of the city, nothing was read on its menswear brands, and there is no evidence that it beats Bicester Village for imported menswear.
  • ICICLE (之禾) and JNBY (江南布衣)Brand names only. Neither was confirmed at a specific 2026 store, so no store is named.
  • The Armament (made-to-measure suits), 412 Madang Road, Xintiandi (马当路412号; Chinese shop name not found)Worth a second look, not researched in depth: a Shanghai government piece (10 Apr 2026, from the Huangpu district WeChat) describes a non-chain made-to-measure suit shop trading for seven years, with Loro Piana, VBC and Holland & Sherry cloth, open 11:00 to 21:00. It is in Xintiandi and open in the evenings, unlike South Bund (closes 18:00). One promotional source only, no prices or independent reviews: ask your hotel front desk or a Chinese-speaking friend to phone first.
  • Heritage tailors Humsuit (亨生, 陕西北路199-201号) and Baromon (培罗蒙, Huashan Road), and the South Maoming Road tailors (玛雅洋服 茂名南路128号; 大卫街洋服 and 森特兰 at 茂名南路59号)Named in the Shanghai commerce commission's 2026 guides only (no prices, hours or independent reviews). Normal bespoke lead time is about three months; Baromon says a rapid suit can be made within a week (The Paper, 16 Feb 2026), so ask first whether the time you have is long enough.
