Kids’ clothing

Where to buy children's clothes in central Shanghai: four malls and department stores with 2026 evidence of operating (a fifth, the Baodaxiang children's store on Nanjing East Road, could not be confirmed open and is listed under 'not recommended'). Start with pick 1; picks 1 and 4 are in the same Xujiahui area and can be one trip. Chinese children's clothes are sized by height in centimetres, not by age, so measure the child's height before you go (the size guide is at the end of this section).

Size guide

Chinese children's clothes are sized by the child's height in cm, not by age. Labels read height/chest for tops and dresses (for example 120/60) and height/waist for trousers (for example 120/53); baby labels may show months (for example 24M). Measure the child, then buy that number or the next size up and try it on, because brands run differently. To turn age into a rough starting size, these are the WHO median heights (boys/girls, cm), with the size to try after each arrow: 6 months 67.6/65.7; 12 months 75.7/74.0 → 80; 18 months 82.3/80.7 → 80 or 90; 2 years 87.1/85.7 → 90; 3 years 96.1/95.1 → 100; 4 years 103.3/102.7 → 100 or 110; 5 years 110.0/109.4 → 110; 6 years 116.0/115.1 → 120; 7 years 121.7/120.8 → 120 or 130; 8 years 127.3/126.6 → 130; 9 years 132.6/132.5 → 130 or 140; 10 years 137.8/138.6 → 140; 11 years 143.1/145.0 → 140 or 150; 12 years 149.1/151.2 → 150. The size to try is the guide's own rounding, a starting point and not a brand chart. Children vary a lot (at age 8 the 3rd to 97th percentile range is about 21 cm), so always go by the measured height. For anything for a child under 36 months or up to 100 cm tall, look for Class A (A类) and the words 婴幼儿用品 (infant and toddler goods) on the tag; Class B is for clothing that touches the skin and Class C for clothing that does not. No children's wholesale or discount market could be confirmed as operating, so this section lists malls and department stores only. How the size guide was checked: the Class A rule was read in the Shanghai regulator's notice; the label examples and the WHO figures are as first reported and have not been confirmed since.

Considered and left out (8)

These were looked at and left out. Each line says why.

  • Baodaxiang Youth and Children's Shopping Centre (宝大祥青少年儿童购物中心), Nanjing East RoadIt could not be independently confirmed as open and trading as described in 2026, so it is not recommended. Look at it only if you are on Nanjing East Road anyway, and check that it is open first.
  • Neon Kids Plaza (上海霓虹儿童广场), 10 Pu'an Road near Jinling Middle Road, HuangpuSmartShanghai marks this underground kids' clothes and toys market as closed (its page was last updated 29 Nov 2023), and no 2025 or 2026 evidence that it reopened was found. Do not plan a visit.
  • Pacific Department Store, 932 Hengshan Road (older guides: kids' clothes on the 7th floor)It closed in August 2023. The site reopened on 22 April 2026 as Neo Young 6 (新六百YOUNG, pick 4), so older guides describe a store that no longer exists.
  • Qipu Road clothing market (七浦路服装批发市场), 168 Qipu Road, Hongkou and Jing'an borderSmartShanghai (updated 26 Jun 2026) lists it as open daily 8:00 to 18:00 but says nothing about children's clothing, and warns of fakes, touts and poor-quality clothes; a 2023 Pengpai report describes the upper floors of a Qipu market as largely shut, with weak sales. No kids' section was confirmed, so it is not worth a special trip for children's wear.
  • Shanghai Takashimaya (上海高岛屋), 1438 Hongqiao Road, Changning (Gubei)Operating status for late 2026 could not be confirmed: its Japanese official page carries no date (and a 2019 closure notice that was later withdrawn), its Chinese site is undated and lists mostly toys and niche labels, and the newest review highlighted on a Wanderlog page is from January 2022. No 2025 or 2026 report was found, so it was left out rather than listed.
  • Kidswant Ultra (孩子王 Ultra), Vanke Mall, 9191 Humin Road, QibaoIt opened on 21 June (the Shanghai government English page is dated 23 Jun 2025), but it is a baby, toy and collectibles superstore far out in Qibao, and the page does not mention clothing.
  • Jing'an Kerry Centre, 4F kids' cluster (Motherswork, i.lollipop, Qkuts)The SmartShanghai entries for these shops were last updated in 2021, and none shows a 2025 or 2026 date. The centre itself is listed as open in July 2026 by the Shanghai government (it shares Jing'an Temple station with Jiuguang), but nothing confirms the kids' shops, so it is left out.
  • Kids' wholesale markets in generalSmartShanghai's list of 32 Shanghai markets contains no children's clothing wholesale market, and no 2025 or 2026 source for one was found. None is claimed.
