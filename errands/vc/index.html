Video conferencing

Shanghai has no street or mall of video-conferencing shops, and none of the global brands (Logitech, Poly, Jabra, Cisco, Yealink) was found to run a public showroom here. What can be visited are experience points run by Chinese brands (Huawei through a dealer, MAXHUB and Hikvision), which are office suites reached by booking, and one new electronics megastore. Meeting-room systems are sold through partners and booked demonstrations rather than over a counter, and the stores in the IT and electronics malls section are general electronics stops: no source names conferencing products at any of them.

How the places connect

MAXHUB and Hikvision are both in Xuhui's Hongmei Road area, about 1.3 km apart in a straight line between street points on OpenStreetMap, so one taxi ride can link them. The Huawei dealer showroom is in outer Minhang, about 3.75 km from the nearest metro station, and JD MALL Qibao is also in Minhang, about 9 km from the Huawei showroom. Less certain here: none of the three experience points says that walk-ins are admitted; only Huawei's gives days and hours (Monday to Friday 9:00 to 17:30), MAXHUB's contact page gives its Shanghai office hours of 9:00 to 18:00 without days, and Hikvision's page was last changed on 21 May 2026 by its server's own date. No source confirms webcams, speakerphones or meeting screens at JD MALL Qibao. Nothing was phoned or visited, and three of the four pins are points on the street, not on the building.

  • Huawei flagship store, East Nanjing Road (华为旗舰店·上海南京东路), HuangpuHuawei's own page for the store (read 17 Nov 2026) lists phones, wearables, PCs, tablets, smart screens (智慧屏), headphones, whole-home products and routers, with hours 周一-周日 10:00-22:00. It does not mention IdeaHub or any meeting-room product; the only commercial-range items are links labelled 商用, 商用产品 and 商用服务 in the header and footer. The store stays in the IT and electronics section as a consumer store, and the dealer showroom among the picks is the place Huawei lists for IdeaHub.
  • Yealink (亿联网络) showroom, ShanghaiYealink's English showroom list (read 17 Nov 2026) shows no Shanghai showroom and no partner showroom in China; its two China entries are online demonstrations run from headquarters that need booking. Its Chinese contact page prints only the Xiamen head-office address.
  • Logitech (罗技) store or showroom, ShanghaiNo Logitech store or showroom in Shanghai was found. Logitech's China business shop page (copyright 2026, read 17 Nov 2026) sends buyers to a reseller search (查找分销商, the page says to contact an authorised partner to buy) and lists no physical shop or Shanghai location.
  • Poly (HP, 惠普) and Jabra (捷波朗), ShanghaiA trade article of 19 May 2021 says Poly had refreshed its Shanghai experience centre but gives no address, and Poly has since become part of HP, whose China contact page (read 17 Nov 2026) lists only an office at 1-6F, Block B, 2727 Jinke Road, Zhangjiang Hi-tech Park. Jabra's first retail experience store in China opened in Xiamen (IT之家, 26 Dec 2023); its China site shows a partner locator and no store list.
  • Cisco (思科) and Microsoft Technology Center, ShanghaiCisco's customer experience centres page (undated, read 17 Nov 2026) lists Bangalore, Singapore and Tokyo for Asia Pacific and no Shanghai centre. Microsoft's locations page (undated, read 17 Nov 2026) lists a Technology Center in Building C9, 77 Hongcao Road, Xuhui, with no visiting rules and no mention of Teams Rooms; it reads as a corporate briefing centre, not a shop.
  • AVer (圆展) and Owl Labs showrooms, ShanghaiNo AVer showroom or store in Shanghai and no Owl Labs seller in mainland China was found (17 Nov 2026).
  • Xylink (小鱼易连) and Kedacom (科达) offices, XuhuiXylink's contact page lists a sales office at 宜山路717号2号楼903室 and Kedacom's a Shanghai office at 虹梅路2007号, neither describing a showroom (read 17 Nov 2026). A 2021 press item, seen only as an excerpt, places a cloud-video experience centre in Xylink's building; it is unconfirmed and may be out of date.
  • Two MAXHUB partner offices (上海敏听智能科技有限公司, 上海志诚泰和信息技术有限公司), Xuhui and Jing'anMAXHUB's experience-point locator (read 17 Nov 2026) lists them with an address and the national hotline only: 上海市徐汇区钦江路102号现代物流大厦西楼15楼 and 上海市静安区共和新路1346号通用大厦201室. Neither is described as a showroom, so both are treated as company offices.
  • Insta360 (影石) authorised experience stores, more than a dozen in ShanghaiThe brand's store-service page (read 17 Nov 2026) lists Shanghai stores such as 上海新天地东台里授权体验店 at 上海市黄浦区吉安路111号新天地东台里 D-L157, with hours 周一至周日 10:00 - 22:00. The page covers service for gimbal, panoramic and wide-angle cameras and the Mic Pro microphone, and does not mention the Link webcam or the Wave speakerphone. They are not video-conferencing showrooms.

  • Aaronsys (上海奥仁视听科技有限公司), 7th floor, South Courtyard, 400 Caobao Road, XuhuiAn integrator that claims a showroom at this address in one company article of 2 Aug 2022; no opening hours or visiting rule is published, and no source confirms that the showroom still exists (the company's contact page and the article were read on 17 Nov 2026). It is a lead for a booked visit, not a place to go.
  • Other Shanghai integrators: Yuliang (上海禹亮信息科技), Zhizhen Shitong (上海直真视通科技), SinowayTheir websites (read 17 Nov 2026) show an office address or an email only, with no showroom, no hours and no invitation to visit: Yuliang names Cisco, Poly, Huawei, Yealink, Neat and MAXHUB among its brands (Room 1107, Block A, 3699 Gonghexin Road, Jing'an) and offers site surveys on request; Zhizhen Shitong (Room 602, Building 1, Tiandi Technology Square, 958 Zhenbei Road, Putuo) offers an online product-trial form; Sinoway publishes guides only.
  • JD city flagship stores (京东电器城市旗舰店) and GOME (国美), ShanghaiWinshang reported on 14 Sep 2023 that JD would open three Shanghai city flagship stores and on 18 Mar 2025 that seven had opened, but no list with street numbers or hours was found and no source describes webcams or meeting equipment in them; JD MALL Qibao is the only JD store with a 2026 opening report. GOME had more than 40 Shanghai stores at its peak and they had basically all closed by Sep 2023 (Winshang).
  • Buynow (百脑汇), XujiahuiAlready in the IT and electronics malls section. Its 2026 reports describe brand laptop counters, a ground-floor phone zone, third-floor PC builders and repair stalls; none mentions webcams, speakerphones or meeting equipment.
  • Trade fairs in Shanghai in the last week of November 2026No fair showing video-conferencing, smart-office or audio-visual hardware was confirmed. The listings read (jufair.com, updated 8 Nov 2026, and shifair.com's December list) show textile and construction machinery (ITMA ASIA 20 to 24 Nov, bauma China 24 to 27 Nov), auto parts, power and metals. The Shanghai Smart Office Technology fair (SSOT, Shanghai New International Expo Centre, 2345 Longyang Road) gives no 2026 dates on its official page; the consumer-electronics and security shows are listed for 9 to 11 Dec; InfoComm China is in Beijing (14 to 16 April 2027); Hongqiao International Tech Week (20 to 22 Nov, National Exhibition and Convention Center) lists about 400 exhibitors on jufair.com, and no source names video-conferencing hardware among them.
