IT malls

Most of Shanghai's old computer malls have closed or been rebuilt, so this section is short on purpose. Buynow in Xujiahui is the one general IT mall confirmed as trading in 2026, Xing Guang is the camera market, and the Apple, Huawei and DJI stores are official places to buy direct from the brand. Prices are at multi-year highs this season after a sharp rise in memory prices, so do not expect bargains on PC parts or gaming laptops. Video-conferencing systems and devices have their own section below.

How the places connect

The DJI store and Apple Hong Kong Plaza are both in or beside Xintiandi, so they fit one short outing. Buynow is in Xujiahui on Line 1, and Apple iapm's nearest station (South Shaanxi Road) is also on Line 1, so iapm and Buynow can be combined. Less certain here: no phone-accessory or gadget market and no PC-parts street market could be confirmed for 2026, no Dianping or Amap ratings could be read, and payment (foreign cards, WeChat Pay or Alipay) and subsidy rules for foreign visitors are unknown at every stop, so carry cash and a card and ask first.

Considered and left out (15)

These were looked at and left out. Each line says why.

  • Pacific Digital Plaza II / TPY Center (太平洋数码广场二期 / TPY中心), XujiahuiThe old PC mall announced its closure in early 2015. The site reopened in April 2024 as TPY Center, a tech, fashion and youth mall with an anime street on level B2. No electronics trading floor was found, so it is no longer an IT hub.
  • Qiujiang Road digital market (虬江路数码交易市场, reported successor 新虬江电子市场, 虬江路500-526号), HongkouThe old Qiujiang Road second-hand market and Shanghai Audio-Video City closed for good around Lunar New Year 2021. A reporter found almost no customers at midday on 13 Jan 2026 in a Qiujiang Road market that sells mainly second-hand goods plus some PC assembly. A low-reliability web answer from January 2026 says it moved to 500-526 Qiujiang Road. It is mostly empty, second-hand and in the north of the city.
  • Da'ao Communications Mall and the Tianmu Road phone markets (大奥通讯商城, 天目西路), near Shanghai Railway StationNo 2025 or 2026 evidence that they still operate. A December 2024 roundup gives 547 Tianmu Xi Lu, but SmartShanghai shows that address as an office and residential block. SmartShanghai's older listing for 457 Tianmu Xi Lu (October 2019, never verified) says new and used phones are mixed, bargaining is expected and returns may be hard, which is a poor fit for a visitor.
  • Shinesun Digital Square (新尚数码广场), 99 Huaihai East RoadSmartShanghai says 'This venue has closed' (listing text of 29 Jun 2020). A December 2024 roundup still lists it with a different address, which looks out of date.
  • CyberMart (赛博数码广场), 1 Huaihai Middle Road near Xizang South RoadIt could not be confirmed either way: there is only a 2014 listing and a December 2024 roundup, and its own website now shows a parked free-hosting notice. A quick map-app check is reasonable, but it is not a planned stop.
  • Super ZhuanZhuan (超级转转), 2066 West Nanjing RoadA 6,000 m2 second-hand warehouse that SmartShanghai says had just opened on 9 Oct 2026. It is mostly designer bags. It also has cameras, headphones and game consoles with QR-code inspection reports, but phones and laptops are not mentioned. It is self-service and some items are barely cheaper than new. It could suit used cameras or consoles, but it is not an IT hub.
  • Metro City (美罗城), 1111 Zhaojiabang Road, XujiahuiOnly one December 2024 roundup says it has electronics, consoles and high-end cameras. No 2025 or 2026 source shows an electronics trading floor, so it is treated as an ordinary mall.
  • Pacific Digital Plaza, Pudong (太平洋数码广场, 张杨路721号)It appears only in one December 2024 roundup that mixes the Xujiahui descriptions with a Pudong address. No listing or 2025 or 2026 report was found for a store at this address, so the address looks wrong.
  • Old 'Buy Now (Xujiahui)' listing at 11 Caoxi Bei Lu (漕溪北路11号)SmartShanghai marks this older listing as closed (listing text of 12 Jun 2019). It is not the Buynow at No. 339 (pick 1), so do not give this address to a driver.
  • Beijing East Road electronics street (北京东路), HuangpuIt was only an unconfirmed lead, and no 2025 or 2026 report of an electronics market there was found. PC parts are now largely bought online; the 2026 reports describe shops quoting from online prices.
  • Wujiaochang mall cluster (五角场), YangpuNo 2025 or 2026 evidence of an electronics trading hub there. Apple has a store at Wujiaochang.
  • Suning Zhaojiabang Road (苏宁易购, 肇嘉浜路955号)A chain appliance and electronics store whose only listing found is from 8 Jun 2019 and was never verified. There is no 2025 or 2026 evidence, and Buynow is nearby.
  • Qingpu Ocean Computer City (青浦海洋电脑城), Jing'an Digital Port (静安数码港), the Fujie, Qiufu and Hengsen markets on Qiujiang Road, and the Gongping Road second-hand marketThey are named only in undated forum answers, with no 2025 or 2026 evidence. Qingpu is in the outer suburbs.
  • Tech showcase stores: Z-Pilot (Xuhui Riverside), MetAI Hub AI Matrix Store (Zhangjiang), Unitree store (Jiuguang, West Nanjing Road)Shanghai's official English site (undated, around May Day 2026) describes them as showrooms for AI glasses, exoskeletons and robots. They are not shopping hubs for PCs, phones or cameras.
  • Huanlong Shopping Center (环龙商场), 3F used-camera shops, next to Shanghai Railway StationAbout two dozen mostly second-hand camera shops (SmartShanghai, 28 Oct 2026, one mention only); address and hours not checked. Xing Guang is the better camera stop.
