Barbers

Five independent barbers and barber-led salons, ranked by how well each one's quality claim and current operation could be checked. They are small shops with few reviews, and none published a 2026 price list, so ask your hotel front desk or a Chinese-speaking friend to phone or message first to confirm the hours, the barber and the price. Ratings come mostly from Amap, a Chinese map app, and were read on 15 Nov 2026.

Notes on these picks

Doc's is the first choice for a visitor who cannot read Chinese: English and Chinese service, a documented straight-razor shave and a shop Instagram that was active in Oct 2026. Doc's, His and the NeXT shop on Shaanxi Nan Road are all near South Shaanxi Road metro station, so they can be compared on one trip. Less certain: no awards, competition results or 2025 to 2026 expat-forum threads were found, and Dianping (China's main review site) blocked access after one page, so the verdict rests on consistent reviews, named barbers and years of operation. Metro stop counts were not checked against a metro map. Doc's may be closed on Mondays (Amap says closed, SmartShanghai says open).

Considered and left out (20)

These were looked at and left out. Each line says why.

  • La Barberia (茂名南路124号, Maoming Nan Road)It describes itself (SmartShanghai) as traditional Italian barbering with modern techniques, but the craft evidence is thin. The main sources are the shop's own SmartShanghai text and four Amap ratings (4.6), three of them dated the same day (2 May 2026), alike, and mostly about hair colour. Amap files the pin under clothing shops, no straight-razor shave or price list was found, and its Instagram tone is street-style rather than classic. It is closed on Mondays, and the name is generic, so search by the address.
  • Truefitt & Hill, Sinan Mansions (特洛菲特·希尔, 思南公馆)An international chain (founded in London in 1805) with one Shanghai shop. A haircut costs CNY 528 (Xinmin Weekly, Dec 2024). It is easy for tourists, but you pay for the brand, and it is not independent.
  • TONI&GUY (托尼盖), Shanghai Centre and other shopsA mall-style chain salon. Choice Shanghai (Oct 2026) labels it a chain, from CNY 280. An older guide also lists a Xintiandi shop on Zizhong Lu (not confirmed since).
  • Franck Provost (Anfu Lu 164) and La Boum / Zorba by La Boum HommeMulti-branch salon brands, not independent barbers. La Boum has at least four sites (Anfu Lu 164, Jing'an Kerry Centre, Xintiandi, Qiantan Taikoo Li) according to Choice Shanghai (Oct 2026).
  • Il Colpo (IFC Pudong) and Il Colpo Bis (iapm)A two-site high-end salon brand, not a barber. A 2017 Time Out list gave cuts from CNY 380 and 280. No 2025 to 2026 evidence was checked.
  • CHANGLE Barbershop (长乐), Fengxian Lu and four other shops in the Juyuanchang areaFive shops (Xinmin Weekly, Dec 2024), about CNY 300, one client at a time with appointments of 45 to 60 minutes. It is well described, but it has more than three branches and no street number or 2025 to 2026 platform evidence was found. It is not independent, though it is the nearest thing to a local brand.
  • Homie Barber Shop (Yan'an Zhong Lu Lane 632) and PartyHead Drinks & Barbers (181-183 Madang Rd, Xintiandi Style; 42 Maoming Nan Lu)The same group, and a Dianping brand page snippet lists 4 Shanghai shops, so it is not independent. PartyHead is a few minutes' walk from Xintiandi metro station (a Jan 2023 blog gave a men's cut at CNY 288 and a wet shave at CNY 188, with WeChat booking), but no 2025 to 2026 evidence was found, so its current status is unconfirmed (not shown to be closed). Ask your hotel front desk or a Chinese-speaking friend to phone first if you want to try PartyHead.
  • D&H by Devilhands (五角场万达店)Multi-branch. Only aggregator pieces mention it.
  • Fade Room 83 (巨鹿路836号, 西康路460号; a Donghu Lu entry)Two or three shops, so not a single independent shop, and atmosphere-first (a 2024-era review says its hot-towel shave is more atmosphere than precision). Active in 2026 (Instagram posts to 19 Sep 2026); SmartShanghai's listings date from 2022.
  • Emperor's Culture Barbershop (巨鹿路158号 Found 158, men only)It has the best craft description of any shop considered (The Paper, Aug 2020: four-pass shave, Chinese-style face shaving around the eyes, nose and ears; cut or shave listed at CNY 498 in 2020). But every detail is from 2020 and no 2025 to 2026 evidence shows it still operates. Unconfirmed, not shown to be closed.
  • Red Rose Barbershop (红玫瑰美发厅, 淮海中路1352号2楼)A state-run heritage shop that reopened on 8 Oct 2023 after renovation (Xuhui government news, Tencent News). No 2025 to 2026 evidence found. SmartShanghai (2020) says the shave was about CNY 40, cash only, and not always smooth or close. A novelty rather than a craft pick.
  • Older state-style shops: Hujiang (沪江), Zhongyuan (中原美发厅, Yandang Lu), Nanjing (南京美发厅)Sources are from 2013 to 2023 and no 2025 to 2026 confirmation was found. Hujiang's original shop closed in 2001 and its offshoots operate under similar names.
  • H.A.N.D Barber Shop (禧记复古理发店, 长安路773号)European barbers and straight-razor shaves, but the SmartShanghai listing was last updated in Mar 2023 and is 'not verified', and the best article is from Dec 2023. No 2025 to 2026 evidence found.
  • 595 Barbershop (南昌路595号, behind iapm)The SmartShanghai listing was last updated in June 2020. No 2025 to 2026 evidence found.
  • Cultura X Grooms & Co (海防路555-2号)Left out because booking is by Instagram message. It is active in 2026 (its Instagram posted to 25 Sep 2026) and is the only shop found that offers a house call. SmartShanghai's listing dates from Aug 2023; no prices seen.
  • Budx The Fixx Barbershop (陕西北路848号)The listing was last updated in Mar 2021 and is unverified. Google Maps showed no reviews. No 2025 to 2026 evidence found.
  • Doc's second shop at Kerry Parkside, Pudong (花木路1388号)Marked closed on SmartShanghai (checked again on 15 Nov 2026). Google Maps still shows a one-review pin, which is likely stale.
  • Neighbourhood lane barbers: 陈程发屋 (lane entrance on Shaanxi Nan Lu, cut CNY 35) and 明丽美发 (Yuanfang Lane behind the Bund)NetEase (Dec 2023) says both operate, but there is no exact address for the first, no English, and nothing from 2025 to 2026. Cheap and plain, not shown to be good.
  • FUXING26 (复兴26), GANO HAIR (加奈美业), 'BARBERSHOP 襄阳南路店'These appear only in templated Weibo and Sina aggregator pieces. FUXING26's 360 Maps address (乌鲁木齐中路358号) does not match the 'Fuxing Road' in the articles. No independent source.
  • L'ARTE SALONE (进贤路128号), De'CelebCut (襄阳南路500号), MicroInk SMP (华山路2号), Gillian Hair Salon (圣平路11号)Not men's barbers: a colour-led salon, an Afro-hair and mobile barber, scalp tattooing, and a salon with no men's service described.
