Payment

Foreign-card acceptance was not tested at any stop, and nothing says whether a foreign card works inside Alipay or WeChat Pay. South Bund stalls mostly prefer cash, WeChat Pay or Alipay and many do not take cards (ATMs and a currency-exchange machine are on site). One 2026 guide says Visa and Mastercard work at most luxury boutiques (Plaza 66), Bicester Village reportedly takes foreign cards, WeChat Pay and Alipay, and small shops on Wukang and Anfu Roads differ. Carry cash as the fallback and ask first: 可以用外国信用卡吗？ (Can I use a foreign credit card?)

Addresses

Every card prints the Chinese address and a show-the-driver line, so show that text on your phone instead of saying it. Watch the lookalikes: IMUSE has two shops (use 泰安路33号 in Xuhui, not 中江路629号 in Putuo); NeXT has two shops with different map pins; HKRI Taikoo Hui is printed with two street numbers (show the mall name 兴业太古汇); Neo Young 6 is 新六百YOUNG, not the old 第六百货; Buynow is 漕溪北路339号, not No. 11. For the barbers and DJI, ask your hotel front desk or a Chinese-speaking friend to phone first.

Hours

Most malls and brand stores list 10:00 to 22:00 daily (Plaza 66 and HKRI Taikoo Hui, Jiuguang, Super Brand Mall, Apple, Huawei, DJI), though some of these come from guides or undated pages, so confirm before a special trip. Buynow is 10:00 to 21:00 (go 11:00 to 20:00 for the upper floors). Bicester is 10:00 to 22:00 Monday to Thursday and 09:30 to 22:00 Friday to Sunday. Two close early: the South Bund fabric market (09:00 to 18:00) and Xing Guang (09:00 to 19:00). Apple has not posted hours for later weeks, and none were found for Grand Gateway 66 or Neo Young 6. Sunset is about 16:50, so do the outdoor Wukang and Anfu walk in daylight, starting in the morning; South Bund (closes 18:00) and Xing Guang (closes 19:00, some shops earlier) need a daytime slot.

Tax refund

The minimum is CNY 200 per receipt, same shop, same day (since April 2025; Xinhua, 2 Jul 2026), not the CNY 500 that one 2026 guide quotes. Many boutiques refund at the till (即买即退): bring your original passport and keep receipts and goods unused for the airport desk. Bicester Village shows 9 percent with an instant refund at its welcome centre; you must leave Shanghai within 28 days, bring a credit card that can be pre-authorised, and keep goods unopened and unused for the customs check. Xing Guang shops do not take part in the scheme, and no refund information was found for South Bund.

Returns, warranty and bargaining

South Bund prices are bargained (China Daily advises bargaining gently). Before any deposit there, get the fabric composition, full price, pickup date and alteration policy in writing, and order made-to-measure rather than ready-made (a 2026 review says ready-made pieces could not be returned). At the kids' malls returns are decided shop by shop, so keep receipts and tags and ask before paying. New camera gear at Xing Guang is not very negotiable (ask for free extras), and customs charges at home should be expected. A mainland-China warranty may not be honoured abroad (not verified), so ask for the terms in writing: 保修在国外有效吗？ (Is the warranty valid abroad?) For a PC build at Buynow, get a same-day written price with every model number (请把配置和型号写下来) and check it online.

Suit timing

A made-to-measure suit at South Bund usually takes 6 to 7 days with a fitting in the middle (Shanghai government, Jan 2026), so order early in a visit, book the second fitting about halfway through and collect before you leave. Faster options are quoted (express in 2 days, about 3 days, next day for urgent orders), but rushing a suit is not advised. The market is open 09:00 to 18:00 daily, so the first visit needs a daytime slot. Suit prices are quoted from about CNY 500 (a cheap two-piece) to 4,000 depending on the cloth, and most sources put a decent suit at CNY 1,000 or more (no price list exists): ask two shops to price the same garment in the same cloth, and check the label, because some cloth sold as wool is polyester.

Barbers

None of the five published a 2026 price list, so ask your hotel front desk or a Chinese-speaking friend to phone or message first to confirm the hours, the barber and the price tier (numbers are on the cards). Doc's (Julu Road; SmartShanghai lists English and Chinese service, untested; WeChat mini-program 'DocsBarberShop') ranks first: go Tuesday to Friday between 12:00 and 20:00, because Monday and weekend hours conflict between sources. For a later cut, IMUSE and His Barber Shop stay open until 21:00, NeXT until 21:00 or 21:30 and Alize until midnight (arrive before 22:00). At Doc's, budget roughly CNY 300 to 600 for a cut plus shave (an estimate only) and ask the tier price before you sit.

Plan by area

(1) Around Xintiandi: DJI, Apple Hong Kong Plaza with a Massimo Dutti flagship in the same mall, and TX Huaihai. (2) South Shaanxi Road, one stop on Line 10: the barbers His and Doc's (Doc's closes 20:00), the NeXT shop on Shaanxi Nan Lu (Amap's pin may be nearer the Yan'an Road end: phone first), and Apple iapm. (3) Xujiahui, three stops further on Line 1: Grand Gateway 66 (kids, 4F North Tower) and Neo Young 6 share the station, and Buynow is a 5 to 10 min walk away. (4) Jing'an: Jiuguang (kids) and Alize salon at Jing'an Temple (Line 14, one stop from South Huangpi Road station, 一大会址·黄陂南路, a separate station from Xintiandi station), then Plaza 66 and HKRI Taikoo Hui on West Nanjing Road (Line 13, two stops from Xintiandi station), about 10 min by taxi from Jiuguang (estimate).

Plan by area, half-days

(1) South Bund fabric market and Xing Guang camera market are daytime-only and both on Line 4, about 10 min by taxi apart (estimate), so one day can cover a suit fitting plus cameras and lenses. (2) Wukang and Anfu Roads: a 3 to 4 hour walk from Jiaotong University station (Line 10, three stops from Xintiandi station) to Changshu Road station, which is two stops from Xujiahui on Line 1; start early, because the Wukang Mansion area is very busy at weekends. (3) Bicester Village: a separate half day, and not two outlets in one day; it is about 20 min by car from Pudong airport, so it can be a stop on the way to or from the airport (no luggage storage found). Super Brand Mall only if you are already in Pudong.

Video conferencing: How it is sold

Meeting-room systems are sold through authorised partners and booked demonstrations, not over a retail counter. Yealink's MeetingBoard page says the product is distributed through authorised business-to-business partners and its showroom list shows no Shanghai showroom; Logitech's business shop page sends buyers to a reseller search; Huawei, MAXHUB and Hikvision each list one Shanghai experience point, and each is an office suite. Several brands also sell online through JD and Tmall. Chinese brand names for a map or shop search: Logitech 罗技, Jabra 捷波朗, HP 惠普, Huawei 华为, Yealink 亿联, Hikvision 海康威视.

Video conferencing: Models and platforms

Ask for the exact model number and the platforms it is certified for (Teams, Zoom, Tencent Meeting, Huawei Cloud Meeting). No source says whether China-market versions of conference cameras, bars or meeting boards differ from international ones or are region-locked. Microsoft's service description for Teams operated by 21Vianet, the China-hosted service (27 Oct 2026), lists Meeting Room devices as not available, so ask the seller to confirm in writing how a unit behaves with a global Teams tenant, and ask for the menu to be switched to English before paying.

Video conferencing: Warranty, invoice and tax refund

No warranty terms for a unit bought in mainland China and used abroad were found, so ask in writing whether the warranty is honoured outside China and who handles claims. Ask for the sales invoice (fapiao, 发票) at the till. Shanghai's official guide (read 17 Nov 2026) says the departure tax refund needs at least 200 yuan at one designated store on one day, the invoice and the goods carried unused through a designated port; it does not say whether meeting-room hardware qualifies, so ask the shop first.

Video conferencing: Power and carrying

Mainland China uses 220 V at 50 Hz with plug types A and I (older buildings still have hybrid A and C sockets), so check the adaptor's label before relying on a travel plug. IATA's passenger guidance (read 17 Nov 2026) says spare batteries are not allowed in checked baggage, so spares and power banks go in the cabin; a speakerphone or headset with a built-in battery follows the airline's own rule, so check before packing.

Video conferencing: Phrases to show

这个有国际版吗？ (Zhège yǒu guójì bǎn ma?) Is there an international version of this? 可以切换成英文界面吗？ (Kěyǐ qiēhuàn chéng Yīngwén jièmiàn ma?) Can the screen language be switched to English? 这个支持 Zoom 或 Teams 吗？ (Zhège zhīchí Zoom huò Teams ma?) Does this support Zoom or Teams? 可以开发票和办离境退税吗？ (Kěyǐ kāi fāpiào hé bàn líjìng tuìshuì ma?) Can you issue an invoice and process the departure tax refund? 保修在国外有效吗？ (Bǎoxiū zài guówài yǒuxiào ma?) Is the warranty valid outside China? The phrases have not been checked by a native speaker.
