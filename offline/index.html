Use it offline

The guide can keep a copy of itself on your phone, so its pages, addresses and search still open when the connection is slow, patchy or blocked. Do this once, on a good connection, before you travel.

What is saved

  • Pages, about 6.7 MB: every place, the lists, the map list, the addresses to show a driver, and search. They are saved by themselves the first time the site opens in Chrome, Safari, Edge or Firefox.
  • Photographs, about 161 MB in all: saved only if you ask below, or one by one as you look at them.
  • Not saved: the map picture, because map tiles cannot be downloaded in bulk, and the Open in Amap links, which need a connection. The map list, Show the driver and Copy address work without one.

When you are online the site always loads its current pages; the saved copy is used only when the connection is slow or missing. Nothing about you is stored or sent: the copy lives in your browser and can be removed below.

Add it to the Home Screen first

iPhone: a Home Screen icon keeps its own saved copy, separate from Safari’s. In Safari tap Share, then Add to Home Screen; open the guide from the new icon once on a good connection, and save the photographs from there. Safari can also clear a site’s saved data after about a week without a visit, so open the icon once more before you travel.

Android, in Chrome: open the menu, then Install app or Add to Home screen; it shares the copy saved in Chrome.
