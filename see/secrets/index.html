How the picks connect

Fuxing Park and the Taofen Memorial are close to each other, near Xintiandi. On a Saturday in late November, Victory Cinema, the last newsstand and Zhapu Road Bridge can make one Hongkou afternoon ending around sunset, about 16:50; Duolun Road is a separate 2.5 km taxi hop and can be added as an optional extra. The marriage market (Saturday or Sunday) and the Fuyou Road book stalls (reported Sundays) need a weekend. Less certain: no verified morning wet market, old-school bathhouse or antiques street, and no rooftop view or quiet temple that locals use could be confirmed for late 2026. Distances are straight-line map estimates, not routed.

Considered and left out (13)

These were looked at and left out. Each line says why.

  • Dongtai Road antique market (东台路古玩市场)Gone: the market was cleared in 2015, and the block reopened in Oct 2025 as the 'Xintiandi Dongtai Li' shopping complex. Old guides still list it.
  • Shunchang Road wet market (顺昌路菜场)No 2025-26 source confirms a market by that name, and the whole block is under redevelopment.
  • Ba Jin's former residence, 113 Wukang Road (巴金故居)No confirmed 2026 opening: one listing says temporarily closed (undated) and others conflict on hours and booking.
  • Shanghai Arts and Crafts Museum, 79 Fenyang Road (上海工艺美术博物馆)Only unofficial listings that conflict on Monday opening; no official 2025-26 notice.
  • Shikumen Open House Museum, Xintiandi North Block (石库门屋里厢博物馆)Closed; the building has been a Le Labo shop and cafe since 2023.
  • Old-school public bathhouseNone verified for 2026. A decade-old article named Lishui Bathhouse (丽水浴室, Fugu Street), but no 2025-26 source was found.
  • Dajing Pavilion and old city wall fragment (大境阁)Closed to visitors; about 30 m of the old wall can be seen from the street.
  • Wenmiao Confucian Temple and its Sunday book market (文庙)The temple suspended opening in Oct 2021 for restoration and no 2026 reopening was found. The market's sellers now trade at Fuyou Road (see the Fuyou Road book market card).
  • Dongjiadu Cathedral (董家渡天主堂)A real parish church, but no 2025-26 local-language report and no check on current visiting rules.
  • World Salon / The Grand Halls (世界会客厅), North BundOpen to the public only by reservation (last Saturday of the month; slots released on the 20th at 12:00, booked by WeChat). Foreigner eligibility is not stated, so it is not practical for you.
  • Rooftop and tower terraces from The Paper's 22 Sep 2026 river-view listThe article gives no addresses, hours or prices, and these are commercial venues not shown to be used by locals. Zhapu Road Bridge, from the same list, was kept instead.
  • Grand Theatre (大光明电影院, 216 West Nanjing Road)Good (1933 building beside People's Park) but famous rather than local, and a late-November rooftop screening is unlikely.
  • Majestic Theatre (美琪大戏院, 66 Jiangning Road)Closed about 10 months from July 2024 for stage upgrades and reopened by May 2025; mainly stage shows, no separate reason to pick it over the Lyceum.
