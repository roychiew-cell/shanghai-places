Historic places

Ten historic places that show how modern Shanghai and modern China took shape, from the Communist Party's founding to the treaty-port Bund and the refugee story. They are ranked by meaning, not convenience, and each card says how sure the check is. Nearly everything closes by 16:30 to 17:00 and on Mondays, so plan for daytime and show the Chinese address to your taxi driver.

How the places connect

The First Congress Site and Old Yuyang Lane are close together and make one morning walk, and the Zhou Residence can be added if its opening is confirmed (Sun Yat-sen's house, close by, has been shut since 15 Nov with no reopening date); the Zhou Residence garden (published winter hours: open to 19:00 until 31 March; check first) is the one regular evening option. The Bund points towards Hongkou and the Jewish Refugees Museum; that walk was not measured. Less certain: lane heritage (only Old Yuyang Lane is picked; Zhangyuan is mostly luxury shops) and several 2026 visiting rules (booking, passports, weekday hours), so ask your hotel front desk or a Chinese-speaking friend to phone ahead where a card says so.

Considered and left out (6)

These were looked at and left out. Each line says why.

  • Zhangyuan (张园), Lane 588 Weihai Road, Jing'anA restored shikumen compound on a late-Qing public garden site, but now mostly international luxury shops, with little history on site and no museum. East zone: first section opened 30 Jun 2026, the rest expected by end 2027, so expect works.
  • Shanghai Bank Museum (上海银行博物馆), 301 Middle Fuxing RoadOpen only Tue, Thu and Sat according to a Nov 2025 government page; a 2023 note says Tue and Thu only. Visits are documented in 2026; current arrangements for individual visitors are unconfirmed.
  • Shanghai Postal Museum (上海邮政博物馆), HongkouA free but specialist postal-history museum, open Tue to Sun since 1 Aug 2025 (Shanghai Post notice of 30 Jul 2025); the address is printed several ways (395 Tiantong Road; 258 or 250 North Suzhou Road) and the rooftop garden was last reported closed in Feb 2025.
  • Shanghai Confucian Temple (上海文庙), 215 Wenmiao RoadLeft out. Reported closed since 8 Oct 2021 for a restoration expected to take 2 to 3 years, and no reopening was found (its website timed out). Do not plan around it without a fresh check. Not independently checked.
  • Memorial of the Fourth National Congress of the CPC (中共四大纪念馆), 1468 North Sichuan Road, HongkouLeft out. Not where the congress met: the Memorial's own page says the original building was destroyed by Japanese shelling in the 1930s. This is a museum in Sichuan North Road Park. Not independently checked.
  • Shikumen Open House Museum (石库门屋里厢博物馆), Lane 181 Taicang Road, XintiandiClosed; the building has been a Le Labo shop and cafe since 2023.
