Streets to walk

Streets of the former French Concession and nearby, suggested for this trip. The tag shows who suggested each. Free to walk and open all day; shop and café hours vary. Wukang and Anfu touch; Julu, Fumin and Changle cross, with Yanqing nearby. Yongjia and Nanchang are farther east and south; Yuyuan and Yongyuan sit near Jing’an Temple. Exact distances between these clusters were not verified.
