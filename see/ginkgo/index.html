Ginkgo avenue · Huangpu

Jumen Road ginkgo stretch 局门路

Walk beneath an avenue of ginkgo trees beside old factory buildings. Yellow leaves were reported here on 26 Nov 2026.

Address
上海市黄浦区局门路（五里桥路至瞿溪路）
Where to look
The ginkgo stretch runs from Wuliqiao Road to Quxi Road. The trees outside Wuliqiao restaurant, Starbucks and Huajian Miyu flower shop are viewpoints named in the tourism bureau’s December 2025 guide.
28 to 30 Nov
Sat 28, Sun 29 and Mon 30 Nov: public street, open 24 hours. Temporary street restrictions have not been checked.
Price
Free street access; purchases extra.
Metro
Nearest station: Shanghai World Expo Museum (世博会博物馆, Line 13), about 500 m in a straight line.
Not verified
Café opening hours and access to the upper floors of No. 8 Bridge creative park have not been confirmed for 28 to 30 Nov.

Riverside ginkgo grove · Pudong

Expo Park ginkgo grove 世博公园

Ginkgo groves beside the river. A 27 Nov report says the leaves are at their best.

Address
上海市浦东新区世博大道1750号（世博公园）
Where to look
Look for the “Ginkgo Gate” (银杏之门) photo spot and a covered bridge, named in the 27 Nov report. Official descriptions also place a grove east of the large lawn.
28 to 30 Nov
For Sat 28, Sun 29 and Mon 30 Nov, aim for 09:00 to 17:00. Published hours disagree: the 2021 city list and Amap say all day; other listings give 09:00 to 19:00 or 09:00 to 17:00. This is a suggested visiting window, not confirmed opening hours.
Price
Free entry, reported on 27 Nov 2026.
Metro
Nearest station: Shibo Avenue (世博大道, Line 13), about 500 m in a straight line to the park pin.
Not verified
The 27 Nov report gives no observation time or photographs. Leaf colour on 28 Nov, gate access and the precise route between the autumn garden and the grove are not confirmed.

Photo: Green Shanghai · not openly licensed

Ginkgo grove and adjoining lawn at Expo Park (世博公园); article published December 2024.

Ginkgo pedestrian street · Changning

Huangjin Chengdao ginkgo walk 黄金城道

Ginkgo trees line this pedestrian street in Gubei, with café windows looking into the branches.

Address
上海市长宁区黄金城道（古北路至伊犁南路）
Where to look
Walk between Gubei Road and Yili South Road. Manner Coffee at 512 Huangjin Chengdao has the second-floor ginkgo window shown in December 2024 coverage.
28 to 30 Nov
Sat 28, Sun 29 and Mon 30 Nov: public street, open 24 hours. The café’s December 2024 listing gave daily hours of 07:30 to 20:30; current hours are not confirmed.
Price
Free street access; coffee extra.
Metro
Nearest station: Yili Road (伊犁路, Line 10), about 500 m in a straight line.
Not verified
The 23 Nov announcement previews the season with promotional wording; it does not report the leaf colour. Manner’s current trading and window seating have not been confirmed.

Photo: Tang Dang, Zhou Yuhua and Tang Zhuoqun / Shanghai Changning · not openly licensed

Ginkgo and the mirrored sculpture on Huangjin Chengdao; article published December 2025.

Registered ancient ginkgo · Putuo

Zhenru Temple 真如寺

An ancient ginkgo spreads over the courtyard between the Hall of Heavenly Kings and the main hall.

Address
上海市普陀区兰溪路399号
Where to look
The tree was assessed as 650 years in 1986. The city tree authority still called it about 650 in October 2025; a December 2024 district report said nearly 700. These are estimates, rather than an exact present-day age.
28 to 30 Nov
For Sat 28, Sun 29 and Mon 30 Nov, arrive after 08:00 and well before 16:00. Listings disagree: Amap says 08:00 to 17:00, last entry 16:00; Putuo District Government’s December 2025 coverage says 06:00 to 16:00. No temple notice for these dates was found.
Price
Old ticket listings disagree at CNY 5 and CNY 10. The current price is not confirmed.
Metro
Nearest station: Tongchuan Road (铜川路, Lines 14 and 15), about 400 m in a straight line. Zhenru (真如, Lines 11 and 14) is useful if you are arriving on Line 11.
Not verified
No reliable November 2026 report of the leaf colour was found.

Photo: Chen Yunxiang / Green Shanghai · not openly licensed

The ancient ginkgo at Zhenru Temple in autumn colour, with the pagoda. File photo, date taken not given; used in an article published October 2025.

Working Chan Buddhist monastery · Xuhui

Longhua Temple and Longhua Pagoda 龙华寺（龙华古寺）· 龙华塔

Ginkgo trees line the square in front of Longhua Pagoda, with more beside Sansheng Hall inside the temple.

Address
上海市徐汇区龙华路2853号
Where to look
Start with the avenue in the pagoda square. December 2025 photographs also show yellow ginkgo beside Sansheng Hall inside the temple.
28 to 30 Nov
Sat 28, Sun 29 and Mon 30 Nov: the undated official tourism listing gives 09:00 to 16:30; SmartShanghai gives 07:00 opening. No notice for these dates was found.
Price
Bendibao (13 Feb 2026) says free temple entry all year. Older guide sources conflict at CNY 10; current terms not confirmed with the temple.
Metro
Longhua (龙华), Lines 11 and 12 (Bendibao, Feb 2026). Exit not newly verified.
Not verified
No reliable November 2026 leaf report was found. Temple entry terms and temporary square restrictions are not confirmed.

Tim Wu / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0

The ornate entrance archway of Longhua Temple, with its gold-lettered 龍華 name board on red, blue side plaques, layered upturned grey-tiled eaves and a large red lantern hanging in the central gateway, with visitors crossing the paved square in front.

Church and neighbouring ginkgo · Huangpu

Holy Trinity Church exterior 圣三一堂

Red-brick church architecture beside a pocket garden with two protected ginkgo trees, reported to be about 150 years old.

Address
上海市黄浦区九江路201号（近江西中路）
Where to look
Look towards the pocket garden at Jiangxi Middle Road and Jiujiang Road, east of the church. The exact pavement view of its ginkgo trees has not been confirmed.
28 to 30 Nov
For Sat 28 to Mon 30 Nov, plan on an exterior stop. Listings show Sunday mornings only: Amap gives 08:00 to 09:00 and 10:00 to 11:00, subject to official notice; Trip.com gives 07:30 to 09:00 and 09:30 to 11:00. The times differ and may be service times. Entry for sightseeing is not confirmed; official tourism lists the church as not open to sightseeing.
Price
Free to view from the public pavement.
Metro
Nearest station: East Nanjing Road (南京东路, Lines 2 and 10), about 300 m in a straight line.
Not verified
No reliable November 2026 leaf report was found. Access to the pocket garden and the church grounds has not been confirmed.

Photo: Yang Jianzheng / Xinmin Evening News · not openly licensed

Ginkgo beside Holy Trinity Church in the Jiangxi Middle Road pocket garden; article published December 2024.

Notes on these picks

Jumen Road and Expo Park are on opposite sides of the river, both served by Line 13. Holy Trinity is a useful extra stop near the Bund. Huangjin Chengdao and Zhenru are separate outings in the west of the city; these six stops do not make a short walking circuit.
