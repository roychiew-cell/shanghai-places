Walk · about 3 hours

Xintiandi and Party-history walk

When
A morning from Tuesday to Sunday, starting about 08:45; never on a Monday
How long
About 3 hours (09:00 to about 12:00)
  1. 09:00Memorial Hall and Site of the First National Congress of the CPC (with the Bowen Girls' School dormitory) 中共一大纪念馆, checkedBe at the door at 09:00. Free, no reservation; bring your passport and expect an airport-style security check (no suitcases, flash or tripods). Allow 1.5-2 h for the museum, the 1920 shikumen house and the Oath Hall. The Bowen Girls' School (127 Taicang Road, 200 m from the Site) is by WeChat booking only, 1-7 days ahead, with passports accepted (booking from a foreign phone number is untested); add 30-45 min if you get a slot.
  2. 11:00Old Yuyang Lane No. 2 (where the Party's first Shanghai group formed) and Huangpu's other hidden Party sites 老渔阳里2号, likelyAbout 11:00 to 11:50. The Printing House corner (Fuxing Middle Road at South Huangpi Road, at Xintiandi station) is a 5-min look. Old Yuyang Lane No. 2 is about 0.7 km from South Huangpi Road station (show 南昌路100弄2号) and needs 20-30 min. Individuals need no booking; entry is free and the site is open Tuesday to Sunday (shut Mondays).
  3. Former Residence of Sun Yat-sen, Shanghai 上海孙中山故居纪念馆Closed from 15 Nov, likelyClosed since 15 Nov 2026 with no reopening date (museum notice of 12 Nov). Skip this stop: the walk ends at Old Yuyang Lane about 11:50.

Closed Mondays. Sun Yat-sen's house has been closed since 15 Nov with no reopening date, so the walk ends at Old Yuyang Lane; for a third stop the Taofen Memorial is nearby (free, Tue-Sun 09:00-16:00).

Walk · about 3 hours

Sinan Road and Fuxing Park evening

When
An evening from Thursday to Sunday, from about 17:45
How long
About 3 hours (17:45 to about 21:00), all on foot
  1. 18:30Zhou Residence: the Communist delegation's Shanghai office (Zhou Enlai, 1946) 周公馆, likelyBe at the gate by 18:30 at the latest: the garden (trial opening) runs 07:00-19:00, last entry 18:30, from 1 Oct to 31 Mar. The house museum closes at 17:00 (last entry 16:30), so in the evening you see the garden and the Sinan Mansions villas around it (about 30 min, estimate); arrive before 16:30 to add the house and the No. 71 exhibition (allow 45-60 min). Free; carry your passport for the ID check at the door.
  2. Fuxing Park (the old French Park): the neighbourhood's dance floor 复兴公园, checkedHave dinner on Sinan Road first (Sinan Mansions cafes surround the Zhou Residence; the park is about 5 min on foot from Sinan Road), then go in from about 19:30. Night amusement area Wed-Sun 19:00-22:00 (rides CNY 30 each or bundles CNY 88/99, not confirmed since); the free retro night train runs Thu-Sun 20:00-24:00 (trial). The park is free (05:00-21:00) and the night park uses the south-west gate. Allow 60-90 min. Taxi address 雁荡路105号.

Ask hotel staff to confirm the Zhou garden is open (a Trip.com 'not operating' flag is unconfirmed) and the night park's winter timetable; use Thursday to Sunday, because the museum is closed Mondays and the night park runs Wed-Sun only.

Walk · about 5.5 hours for the afternoon version

Bund, ferry and Shanghai Tower: the river evening

When
An afternoon from 14:45 (the sunset version), or an evening from about 17:30 (the version in the note)
How long
About 5.5 hours for the afternoon version (14:45 to about 20:30); about 4 to 4.5 hours for the evening version
  1. The Bund at dusk 外滩, checkedStart at Waibaidu Bridge (show the driver 外白渡桥); look for the look-alike rivet heads from the 2008-09 repair. Walk south along the promenade past the Peace Hotel (No. 20), Customs House (No. 13) and old HSBC (No. 12) in the late-afternoon light (sunset 16:52), reaching the reopened Bund Signal Tower at the south end by about 16:45 (10:00-22:00 per two posts: check on arrival; in 1993 it was moved 22.4 m in one piece). Allow 1.5-2 h. Dress warmly: 5-14 C with a river wind.
  2. Dongjin ferry: Bund to Lujiazui for CNY 2 东金线轮渡, checkedThe Jinling East Road ferry terminal is a few minutes' walk further south of the Signal Tower (show 金陵东路渡口); board about 17:00-17:15 for blue hour on the water; boats run about every 15 min, last 18:30 from the Bund and 18:15 from Pudong. The crossing takes about 10 min and costs CNY 2: pay with an Alipay or WeChat transport code, the Suishenxing app, a transit card or cash (a foreign contactless bank card cannot tap the gate). Then walk the Lujiazui riverside for the lit Bund across the water (about 25 min, estimate).
  3. 18:15Shanghai Tower observatory, 'Top of Shanghai' (118th floor) 上海中心大厦, checkedBook a timed slot for about 18:15: real-name ticket, CNY 180, on Trip.com or the tower's English website, booked the day before because fog can block the view. From the ferry walk about 10-15 min to Lujiazui station, then about 10 min to the tower (Exit 9, B2 walkway). Allow 2-2.5 h in all; one hour on the deck is plenty. Hours: daily 08:30-22:00, last admission 21:30 (Trip.com, labelled 2025), so check the time on your ticket.

Evening version: leave about 17:30, skip the ferry and the sunset (last boats 18:15 and 18:30) and take a tower slot at about 19:30; the lights are expected on about 18:00 (inference). The Bund and the tower are open daily, so Monday also works.

Walk · about 5 hours

Hongkou refugee-history afternoon, ending at sunset

When
An afternoon, starting about 12:45 (the museum is closed Mondays)
How long
About 5 hours (12:45 to about 18:00)
  1. Shanghai Jewish Refugees Museum (Ohel Moshe Synagogue) 上海犹太难民纪念馆, checkedStart at the museum (show the driver 上海市虹口区长阳路62号). CNY 20; open Tue-Sun 09:00-17:00, last ticket sale and entry 16:00; the ticket is real-name, so bring your passport. Allow 1.5-2 h inside (the 14:00 free talk is Chinese only), then about 30 min on Zhoushan and Huoshan roads for the exteriors of refugee-era sites: the old Joint Distribution Committee office (119-121 Huoshan Road) and Huoshan Park's monument.
  2. Shanghai's last newspaper shop, Zhapu Road (moved there 1 Jan 2026) 上海最后一家报刊门市部, likelyTake a taxi from the museum to 430 Zhapu Road (show 乍浦路430号), about 15 min (estimate). Reported open 06:00-18:00 (Jiefang Daily says it closes at 17:30), so arrive before 17:00; allow 20-30 min. Buy something, ask before photographing the owner or customers, and carry some cash. Check the Amap listing first: nothing later than March 2026 was found.
  3. Victory Cinema and Duolun Road: a Hongkou afternoon with a story 胜利电影院, likelyOnly the cinema's exterior (10 min): it is at 408 Zhapu Road, about 200 m from the newspaper shop. Screenings are unconfirmed, so look at the facade and any posted programme. Skip the Duolun Road half: it is a separate 2.5 km taxi hop, and the section of Duolun Road between Sichuan North Road and Lane 201 is closed to vehicles 11:00-18:00 on some weekend days (see the Taxis note; authorised vehicles excepted).
  4. Zhapu Road Bridge: the photographers' bridge over Suzhou Creek 乍浦路桥, checkedWalk south about 0.7 km (the cinema's card puts it 0.7 km north of the bridge; about 10 min on foot, estimate) to be on the bridge by 16:40; sunset is 16:52 and civil dusk 17:18, and you can stay to about 17:30. Free, no gate. Stand on the pavement mid-bridge and look east to Waibaidu Bridge and the Lujiazui towers; no tripods in the walkway, and wrap up against the wind.
  5. The Bund at dusk 外滩, checkedOptional finish: walk 250 m east to Waibaidu Bridge (look for the look-alike rivet heads from the 2008-09 repair) and on along the promenade for the lights, expected about 18:00 (inference). Then take a taxi or Line 10 from East Nanjing Road.

Closed Mondays; the newspaper shop is best before 16:00 and the bridge at 16:40, so if the museum runs long drop the lanes and the cinema.

Walk · about 6.75 hours

Old city half day: Yu Garden, the marriage market and old books

When
A Sunday, 09:00 to about 15:45 (06:30 start if you add the dawn book market); never Monday, when Yu Garden is closed
How long
About 6.75 hours (09:00 to about 15:45) including lunch; add about 2 hours for the optional dawn book market
  1. Second-hand book stalls at Fuyou Building B2 (check before going) 福佑商厦地下2层旧书摊, unconfirmedOptional, Sunday only, and only if your hotel front desk or a Chinese-speaking friend confirms it is trading and when (current trading is unconfirmed and the latest report found is dated 28 Apr 2026; ask them to check Amap or phone the day before; an April report says the afternoon is busier). Show the driver 福佑路225号福佑商厦, B2. Bring small cash and ask before photographing sellers. Allow 1.5-2 h; the garden's North Gate is also on Fuyou Road (168 Fuyou Road).
  2. 09:00Yu Garden and the old city bazaar 豫园, checkedBe at the gate at 09:00 (Sunday is the busiest day; open 9:00-16:30, last entry 16:00). CNY 40 in late November; carry your original passport. Metro: Line 10 to Yuyuan Garden station, then 8 min on foot. Allow 1-1.5 h in the garden, then about 1 h in the free bazaar lanes and City God Temple streets, and lunch there (tourist prices).
  3. People's Park marriage market (parents' matchmaking corner) 人民公园相亲角, likelyLeave about 12:30 for a taxi of about 12 min (estimate) to 人民公园 (南京西路231号). The market runs Saturday and Sunday about 12:00-17:00, informally (arrive 12:30-16:00). Watch only, free; allow 45-90 min. Do not photograph a sheet or a face without asking, say 不用了 (bù yòng le, 'no thanks') to brokers, and never give out your WeChat or passport details. It is cold by late afternoon.
  4. 14:15Shanghai Guji (Ancient Books) Bookstore, with the Art Bookshop vinyl corner opposite 上海古籍书店, checkedAbout 14:15 to 15:45, after a short hop of about 5 min by taxi (estimate) to 福州路401号 (People's Square is about 0.7 km away). Free to enter; allow about 90 min for both shops. Hours are unofficial (10:00-20:00 daily, arrive by about 17:30), so check the Amap listing. Almost all titles are Chinese: go for the building, the facsimile Yongle Encyclopedia wall (1F) and the 3F second-hand floor.

Yu Garden is closed Mondays, the book market runs Sundays only and the marriage market weekends only. To go on to the Bund, end after the marriage market (about 14:00). For a performance instead of the marriage market, swap in Yalu Storytelling Hall (13:30-15:30, CNY 10 at the door, room 709, spoken in dialect with no subtitles; about 10 min by taxi from Yu Garden (estimate)).
