Historic places

10 places

They are ranked by meaning, not convenience, and each card says how sure the check is. Nearly everything closes by 16:30 to 17:00 and on Mondays, so plan for daytime and show the Chinese address to your taxi driver.

How the places connect

The First Congress Site, Old Yuyang Lane, Sun Yat-sen's house and the Zhou Residence are close together and make one morning walk; the Zhou Residence garden (open to 19:00 until 31 March) is the one regular evening option. Thin spots: lane heritage (only Old Yuyang Lane is picked; Zhangyuan is mostly luxury shops) and several 2026 visiting rules (booking, passports, weekday hours), so ask your hotel front desk or a Chinese-speaking friend to phone ahead where a card says so.

14 facts

Fourteen stories, each tied to a place you can go and look at, best first. Most are documented; one is widely reported and one is a legend. Each card says what could not be checked, so read the caveat before you plan a visit, and confirm hours and booking on the day.

Did you know

In 2003 the 1930 Shanghai Concert Hall (about 5,650 tonnes) was lifted 3.38 m and slid 66.46 m southeast with 59 hydraulic jacks, away from the noise and exhaust of the Yan'an Elevated Road. It reopened on 1 Oct 2004.

Stand outside by the elevated road and picture the whole theatre being lifted and slid sideways; it still hosts concerts.

Shanghai Concert Hall (Cadillac Shanghai Concert Hall), 523 Yan'an East Road, Huangpu (free to see from outside)Documented
Caveat and sources

Tonnage and jacks are confirmed by a 2003 wire report (The Star) and Yicai (2024). English Wikipedia's version (moved 2007, 5,800 t) is wrong; zh-Wikipedia gives 1 Jan 2005 for the whole project's end. A 2026 season programme lists Steven Osborne on 29 Nov (single-source); confirm on the hall's ticketing.

Did you know

The Waibaidu Bridge (Garden Bridge), opened 20 Jan 1908, is usually billed as China's first all-steel riveted bridge. In 2008-09 it was floated off on barges for about ten months of repairs, then floated back.

Look for the rivet heads on the steel truss: about 63,000, roughly 40% of the total, were replaced with look-alike copies in the 2008-09 repair.

The Bund at dusk 外滩（中山东一路） Documented
Caveat and sources

'First' is a local boast: a steel railway bridge over the Yellow River at Zhengzhou opened in Nov 1905. Dates vary by source (1907 or 1908). About 63,000 of roughly 160,000 rivets were replaced (Hongkou district account). It is a working road; a Sep 2026 notice closed it to vehicles only for a parade.

Did you know

The Chinese Communist Party's official birthday, 1 July, is not the day its First Congress opened: that was 23 July 1921, in a Shanghai lane house. Police searched it on 30 July; the delegates moved to a boat in Jiaxing.

The date 1 July was fixed later (Central Committee, June 1941); the meeting opened in a house in Xintiandi.

Memorial Hall and Site of the First National Congress of the CPC (with the Bowen Girls' School dormitory) 中共一大纪念馆 · 中共一大会址（含中共一大代表宿舍旧址／博文女校旧址） Documented
Caveat and sources

Did you know

Xintiandi's shikumen lane houses grew out of refugee booms: after the 1853 Small Swords uprising, more than 20,000 Chinese lived in the foreign concessions by 1854-55. Brick lane houses with stone-framed doors replaced timber huts.

Walk the lanes and look at the stone-framed doors: by the 1930s this house type sheltered roughly half to two-thirds of Shanghai's residents.

Xintiandi shikumen lanes (North Block off Taicang Road, South Block around Madang Road), Huangpu, a few minutes from Xintiandi metro stationDocumented
Caveat and sources

Name: usually explained as 石箍门 (stone-hooped door), since 箍 and 库 sound alike locally; historians give two accounts. Xintiandi was extensively reconstructed. Did you know

Shanghai's old central street grid is a map of China: the British consul proposed in 1862 that north-south streets take province names and east-west streets city names. In 1865 the Municipal Council adopted Chinese place names for 27 roads.

Look at the street signs as you walk west from the Bund: Jiangxi, Sichuan and Guangxi Roads are provinces, Nanjing and Beijing Roads are cities.

Did you know

Yan'an East Road follows a filled-in creek, the Yangjingbang, once the border between the British Settlement and the French Concession. Filling began in 1914; the road was named Avenue Edward VII in 1916.

The creek's name became the term for pidgin English (洋泾浜英语); rickshaws could not cross the border, and voltage differed (220 V British, 110 V French).

Did you know

Shanghai Tower, 632 m and 128 floors, twists 120 degrees from bottom to top. By designer Gensler's own figure the twist cuts wind loads by about 24% versus a rectangular tower, saving about US$58 million in materials.

From the Bund, look for the spiral of the outer glass skin and the V-shaped notch (strake) sliding up one side.

Did you know

Shanghai has the world's first commercial high-speed maglev line: about 30 km from Longyang Road station to Pudong Airport in about 8 minutes. Its design speed is 431 km/h, but regular service is held to about 300 km/h.

Did you know

The one-line 'No dogs or Chinese allowed' sign at Huangpu Park is a legend. What is documented: 1885 park rules banned dogs and bicycles and, separately, Chinese except servants; Chinese were admitted from 1 June 1928.

Historians separate what the posted rules said from the catchy one-line sign, which also appears in the 1972 film Fist of Fury.

Practical notes

10 notes
Closing days

Mondays shut the First Congress Memorial and Site, the Jewish Refugees Museum, Sun Yat-sen's and Soong Ching-ling's houses, the Zhou Residence museum, the Taofen Memorial, Yu Garden, the Power Station of Art, Lu Xun's house; Shanghai Museum East is closed Tuesdays. Open on Mondays: the Bund, Fuxing Park, Zhapu Road Bridge, Jade Buddha Temple (daily 08:00-16:30) and Shanghai Tower (daily 08:30-22:00 per Trip.com, labelled 2025; check the time on your ticket).

Passports

Carry your original passport every day. It is asked for at the First Congress Memorial (security), the Zhou Residence (ID or fingerprint at the door), Yu Garden, Shanghai Museum East's East Gate and the Jewish Refugees Museum (real-name ticket). The Shanghai Tower ticket is real-name and timed, and the passport name must match it exactly.

Book ahead

Book Shanghai Tower the day before, because fog can block the view. The Bowen Girls' School needs a WeChat mini-program booking 1-7 days ahead and a foreigner may be unable to book it.

Payment and cash

The Dongjin ferry (CNY 2) takes an Alipay or WeChat transport code, the Suishenxing app, a transit card or cash, but not a foreign contactless bank card. Yalu (CNY 10 at the door), the Fumin Building market (many books start at CNY 1, CNY 3-10 is typical) and the last newspaper shop are small-cash places; Yu Garden tickets are sold at the gate window or in the 'Shanghai Yuyuan' mini-program.

Taxis

Show the driver the Chinese address from each card, for example 请带我去中共一大纪念馆，黄陂南路374号 (First Congress Memorial), 上海市虹口区长阳路62号 (Jewish Refugees Museum) or 福佑路223号 (Fumin Building). On Sat 28 and Sun 29 Nov Duolun Road in Hongkou is closed to vehicles 11:00-18:00: ask the taxi to stop on Sichuan North Road.

Weather and daylight

About 5-14 C with a cold river wind at the Bund; sunset is about 16:50 (16:52 on 28 Nov, civil dusk 17:18). Museums and gardens mostly close between 16:00 and 17:00, with last entry at 16:00-16:30, so daytime sights need a start between 09:00 and about 14:00.

Evenings

After sunset the cards support the Bund promenade (open 24 hours), the Signal Tower (10:00-22:00, from two posts), Shanghai Tower (to 22:00, last admission 21:30), the Zhou Residence garden (to 19:00, last entry 18:30), the Fuxing Park night park (Wed-Sun 19:00-22:00) and Guji Bookstore (unofficial 10:00-20:00). Yalu, the museums and the marriage market are daytime only.

Crowds

Weekends are busiest at the First Congress Memorial (afternoons), Yu Garden and the Bund (29,112 people on the waterfront at 16:00 on 1 Oct 2026, a holiday), so go at opening time. Shanghai Tower weekend walk-up queues can reach about 2 hours (one guide), and its sunset slot sells out first.

Phone ahead

Ask your hotel front desk or a Chinese-speaking friend to check on Amap, or phone, the day before for places the cards flag as unconfirmed: the Fumin Building book market (low confidence it still runs), Sun Yat-sen's house (a repair project is budgeted), the Zhou Residence (a Trip.com 'not operating' flag), the Signal Tower's hours and price, and the Fuxing Park night park's winter timetable.

People and estimates
