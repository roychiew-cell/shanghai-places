Historic places that matter

Ten historic places that show how modern Shanghai and modern China took shape, from the Communist Party's founding to the treaty-port Bund and the refugee story. They are ranked by meaning, not convenience, and each card says how sure we are. Nearly everything closes by 16:30 to 17:00 and on Mondays (Mon 30 Nov), so plan for daytime, ideally Sat 28 or Sun 29 Nov, and show the Chinese address to your taxi driver.

1

Memorial Hall and Site of the First National Congress of the CPC (with the Bowen Girls' School dormitory) 中共一大纪念馆 · 中共一大会址（含中共一大代表宿舍旧址／博文女校旧址）

Museum, original 1920 shikumen house and restored dormitory, run as one memorial · Xintiandi, Huangpu District

Confirmed

On 23 July 1921, thirteen delegates (some sources say twelve) opened the Chinese Communist Party's first national congress in this rented shikumen house in the French Concession. A police raid on 30 July ended the meeting here, and the delegates finished on a boat at Jiaxing. The modest house shows how small and improvised the start was.

From the hotel
About 0.7 to 0.8 km on foot, roughly 9 to 10 min; the Bowen Girls' School is about 12 min. No taxi needed. Map estimates, not re-measured.
Metro
Xintiandi (Lines 10 and 13) and South Huangpi Road (Lines 1 and 14). Station signs now read 一大会址·新天地 and 一大会址·黄陂南路.
Hours
Memorial and Site: Tue-Sun 09:00-17:00, last entry 16:30; closed Monday except public holidays (incl. Mon 30 Nov). Free guided talks at set times. Bowen Girls' School: timed visits only.
Tickets and booking
Memorial and Site: free, no reservation (official page). Bowen Girls' School: reservation only, 1 to 7 days ahead in the WeChat mini-program 中共一大纪念馆; fee not stated.
Time needed
About 1.5 to 2 hours for the museum and house; add 30 to 45 minutes for the Bowen Girls' School (estimates).
More: what to see, how to, tips and cautions
What to see
The original 1920 shikumen house, its meeting room laid out as in 1921; the Oath Hall; the museum 'Epoch-making Beginnings' (opened 2021, English signs). At the Bowen Girls' School (博文女校, reopened March 2026), delegates' rooms are recreated.
How to
Show: 请带我去中共一大纪念馆，黄陂南路374号. Expect an airport-style security check; no suitcases, flash or tripods. Bring your passport.
Best time
Arrive at 09:00 or after 15:00. Weekend afternoons are busiest, and on 1 Oct 2026 crowds queued from about 08:00. Closed Mon 30 Nov.
Local tip
Do the Bowen Girls' School first (book ahead), then walk 200 m to the Site. It reopened on 24 March 2026 after restoration.
Watch out
Bowen Girls' School needs a WeChat booking; a foreign visitor may be unable to book, and no walk-in rule is published. Summer evening openings (Jul-Aug 2026) were one-offs: assume 17:00 closing. The exhibition is the Party's own account; the meeting room is a reconstruction.

Shanghai Jewish Refugees Museum (Ohel Moshe Synagogue) 上海犹太难民纪念馆（摩西会堂旧址）

Museum in a restored 1927 synagogue · Tilanqiao historic area, Hongkou District

Confirmed

From the mid-1930s about 20,000 European Jews fled Nazi persecution to Shanghai, an open port where no visa was needed before 1941. In February 1943 the Japanese authorities ordered stateless refugees into a Hongkou area of under 3 sq km that already housed about 100,000 Chinese. Most refugees survived the war.

From the hotel
Not walkable: about 7 km by road. Taxi roughly 20 to 30 min off-peak; metro with one change about 35 to 45 min (estimates).
Metro
Line 12, Tilanqiao (提篮桥), Exit 1. From Xintiandi: Line 10 four stops to Tiantong Road (天潼路), then Line 12 two stops.
Hours
Tue-Sun 09:00-17:00; last ticket sale and entry 16:00; closed Monday. A Nov 2026 reseller table shows Mondays 2, 9, 16, 23 and 30 Nov closed.
Tickets and booking
Adult CNY 20; 60+ CNY 15; student CNY 10 (23 and under, with ID). Free: children 1.3 m and under, disabled, military, on-duty police, martyrs' relatives.
Time needed
About 1.5 to 2 hours inside (estimate); about 3 hours with the surrounding lanes.
More: what to see, how to, tips and cautions
What to see
The restored 1927 Ohel Moshe synagogue; a six-part exhibition on the escape, life in refuge, the 1943 designated area and departure; the Wall of Names (19,672 names since 21 Oct 2025) with a searchable database.
How to
Bring your passport (real-name ticket). Show the driver 上海市虹口区长阳路62号. Buy tickets on Meituan, Dianping or Ctrip, or at the window.
Best time
Weekday morning. The free talks at 10:00 and 14:00 are in Chinese only, per the official guide. Arrive well before the 16:00 last entry.
Local tip
Walk nearby Zhoushan and Huoshan roads, where the museum lists refugee-era sites (mostly exteriors): the old Joint Distribution Committee office (119-121 Huoshan Road) and Huoshan Park's 1994 monument.
Watch out
Closed Mondays (Mon 30 Nov). Free talks are Chinese only. Sources differ on numbers (18,000 to 23,000 in the area) and the end date: Wikipedia says liberated 3 Sep 1945; the museum gives no date, and Japan announced surrender on 15 Aug.

The Bund (old International Settlement waterfront) and the reopened Bund Signal Tower 外滩 与 外滩信号台

Historic riverfront (52 buildings) plus a 1907 signal tower reopened in Oct 2026 · Huangpu District, west bank of the Huangpu River

Confirmed

After the First Opium War Shanghai became a treaty port (1842-43). The British settlement of 1845 and the American one merged into the International Settlement in 1863, and its riverfront became the Bund. By the 1920s it was East Asia's financial street; the HSBC Building (1923) and Custom House (1927) are its symbols.

From the hotel
Not a walk: about 3.5 km by road. Taxi 12 to 20 min; Line 10 three stops from Xintiandi, 10 to 12 min plus a 10-minute walk (estimates).
Metro
East Nanjing Road (南京东路, Lines 2 and 10) suits the middle Bund; Yuyuan Garden (豫园, Line 10) is nearer the Signal Tower (about 1 km walk).
Hours
Promenade: 24 hours. Signal Tower: 10:00-22:00 per two self-media posts (19 Oct 2026); Guangming Daily gives no hours, so check on arrival. HSBC lobby: unknown.
Tickets and booking
Promenade free. Signal Tower price and booking rule: not stated. HSBC lobby: a Sep 2026 list says free on workdays; 2022 articles say banking customers only.
Time needed
1.5 to 2 hours for the walk and the Signal Tower; add 30 minutes if the HSBC lobby admits visitors.
More: what to see, how to, tips and cautions
What to see
Bund Signal Tower: floor 1 visitor centre, Bund history exhibition and shop; floor 2 cafe with river views. Then walk north past the HSBC Building, Custom House and Peace Hotel to Waibaidu Bridge (1908).
How to
Taxi: show 外滩, or 黄浦区中山东二路1号甲 for the tower. Metro Line 10 to East Nanjing Road or Yuyuan Garden. Start at the tower and walk north.
Best time
See Classic sights, card 1 (The Bund at dusk), for the plan: arrive about 16:15 to 16:30, sunset is about 16:50, then the lit skyline after dark. Weekday evenings are quieter. The river wind is cold in late November.
Local tip
The tower reopened in October 2026: a fresh first stop. From the north end, Waibaidu Bridge leads into Hongkou, where the Jewish Refugees Museum is (distance not checked).
Watch out
Tower sources differ: first station 1883 or 1884 (English Wikipedia: a French one in 1865); signalling ended 1956 or Feb 1957. The 'No dogs and Chinese' sign is a legend; the Huangpu Park sign had ten rules, rule 1 reserving it for foreigners.

Former Residence of Sun Yat-sen, Shanghai 上海孙中山故居纪念馆

Historic house museum · Xiangshan Road beside Fuxing Park, Huangpu District

Likely

In 1918 four overseas Chinese in Canada bought this house for Sun Yat-sen, who had no fixed home. He lived and worked here until late 1924 and met Soviet envoy Joffe; the museum's account ties the 1923 Sun-Joffe manifesto, which opened the way to Soviet help, to this house. His widow Soong Ching-ling lived here until 1937.

From the hotel
About 1 km, roughly 12 to 15 min on foot; taxi about 5 min (estimates; the house's map point was approximate).
Metro
Line 13 Middle Huaihai Road (Exit 1); Lines 10 and 13 Xintiandi (Exit 6); Lines 1, 10 and 12 South Shaanxi Road (Exit 6).
Hours
Tue-Sun 09:00-17:00, ticket sales stop 16:30; closed Mondays except public holidays (so closed Mon 30 Nov).
Tickets and booking
CNY 20 adult; CNY 10 students and 60+ (with ID); under-18s and 70+ free. No booking needed: listed as walk-in by City News Service (9 May 2026).
Time needed
45 to 60 minutes (estimate; the house is small).
More: what to see, how to, tips and cautions
What to see
A small, furnished two-storey European-style house: living and dining rooms below, study and bedroom above; about 5,000 books, maps (two drawn by Sun), a 1917 command sword; a statue of Sun outside.
How to
Show the driver: 请带我去香山路7号孙中山故居. Carry your passport. Check the committee's page, or have the hotel call, the day before.
Best time
Go at opening (09:00); the small rooms feel crowded at weekends. Closed Mon 30 Nov.
Local tip
It sits beside Fuxing Park, and the Zhou Residence is a 5- to 10-minute walk away, so do both in one trip.
Watch out
A 2026-27 repair and exhibition-revamp project (RMB 12.6 m) is budgeted for the house; no closure notice was found, so check before going. Sun left Shanghai in late 1924 and died in Beijing on 12 March 1925. 'Manifesto drafted here' is the museum's account.

Xujiahui: St Ignatius Cathedral, Tushanwan Museum and the Xu Guangqi sites 徐家汇（徐家汇圣依纳爵主教座堂·土山湾博物馆·徐光启纪念馆）

Jesuit-era heritage cluster: 1910 cathedral, museum, memorial hall, 1847 library · Xujiahui, Xuhui District

Likely

Xujiahui is where Jesuit-led Western learning took root in Shanghai: from 1847 a library (Wikipedia: the city's first modern one), a school, the Tushanwan orphanage workshops and an observatory (1873). Xu Guangqi (1562-1633), baptised in 1603, worked with Matteo Ricci on part of Euclid and led the 1629 calendar reform. The cathedral dates from 1906-1910.

From the hotel
Not walkable: about 6 km by road. Taxi about 20 to 30 min; Line 1 four stops to Xujiahui, about 20 min plus a 10-minute walk (estimates).
Metro
Xujiahui (徐家汇, Lines 1, 9 and 11). Line 1 from South Huangpi Road (now signed 一大会址·黄陂南路): South Shaanxi Road, Changshu Road, Hengshan Road, Xujiahui.
Hours
Tushanwan Museum: Tue-Sun 09:00-16:30, last entry 16:00 (government page, Nov 2025); closed Mon 30 Nov. Cathedral sightseeing hours not published. Xu Guangqi hall: unconfirmed (about 2019: Tue-Sun 09:00-16:30).
Tickets and booking
Free for the cathedral, Tushanwan Museum, Xu Guangqi hall and library (government page, 20 Nov 2025). The observatory museum needs a WeChat reservation and is hard to book.
Time needed
2 to 3 hours for the cathedral, Tushanwan Museum and one more site (estimate).
More: what to see, how to, tips and cautions
What to see
St Ignatius Cathedral: red-brick Gothic, twin 57 m towers, consecrated 1910, restored by Dec 2017. Tushanwan Museum (open since June 2010): carving, painting, stained glass, an orphan-built pailou gate. Xu Guangqi's memorial hall is in Guangqi Park.
How to
Metro Line 1 to Xujiahui. Taxi: show 上海蒲西路158号 for the cathedral. Do not walk in during Mass. Ask your hotel to check Tushanwan's hours first.
Best time
Weekday before 16:00 (museum last entry 16:00). The cathedral's east-facing facade is best lit in the morning. Avoid Mass times.
Local tip
Visit Tushanwan Museum first (indoors, free) and the cathedral last. The 12:00 Sunday Mass is the one service the diocese lists in English.
Watch out
Cathedral hours for sightseers are not published; on Sundays non-Catholics may be turned away (unverified traveller note). Xu Guangqi hall hours are unconfirmed. Tushanwan has been open since June 2010 (Wen Wei Po); English Wikipedia's 2020 date conflicts with that.

Lu Xun's last home (Dalu New Village No. 9) and Lu Xun Park 上海鲁迅故居（山阴路132弄9号，大陆新村9号）· 鲁迅公园（鲁迅墓、上海鲁迅纪念馆）

Preserved writer's house, plus a park with his tomb and memorial hall · Hongkou District, north of Suzhou Creek

Confirmed

Lu Xun (1881-1936), founder of modern Chinese vernacular literature, spent his last nine years in Shanghai writing essays against the Nationalist censors and co-founded the League of Left-Wing Writers in 1930. He lived in this rented house from 11 April 1933 and died here on 19 October 1936. His tomb in the park carries Mao Zedong's calligraphy.

From the hotel
Far: about 7 to 8 km by road. Taxi about 25 to 30 min; metro with a change about 40 min (estimates).
Metro
Lines 3 and 8, Hongkou Football Stadium, for the park. The house on Shanyin Road is about 0.7 km from the park (estimate).
Hours
House: 09:00-16:00, closed Mondays (Shanghai government, 31 Aug 2026). Memorial Hall: Tue-Sun 09:00-17:00, last entry 16:30 (2025 listing). Park: 05:00-21:00 (Nov-Apr).
Tickets and booking
House: CNY 8 on older listings, unconfirmed for 2026; entry in small timed groups. Park and Plum Garden free; Meixuan CNY 15. Memorial Hall: free per older sources.
Time needed
House: 30 to 40 minutes. Park, tomb and Memorial Hall: 1 to 1.5 hours (estimates).
More: what to see, how to, tips and cautions
What to see
House: original furniture and his second-floor study-bedroom, with a calendar and clock stopped at his death. Park: his tomb, the Memorial Hall, the Plum Garden (free to stroll) and the Meixuan building's exhibition on Yun Bong-gil (CNY 15).
How to
Show: 请带我去山阴路132弄9号鲁迅故居 (house) or 鲁迅公园 (park). Taxi is easier than the metro. The house is small; ask your hotel to phone ahead at weekends (2020 advice).
Best time
Weekday morning. If you add Duolun Road (about 1 km south of the park), note it is closed to vehicles 11:00-18:00 on Sat 28 and Sun 29 Nov: ask the taxi to stop on Sichuan North Road. Taxis to the park and the house are not affected.
Local tip
On 29 April 1932 Korean independence fighter Yun Bong-gil threw a bomb at a Japanese military celebration in this park (then Hongkew Park); the Meixuan building commemorates him.
Watch out
Park address differs by source: 2288 North Sichuan Road (Shanghai government, 2026) or 146 East Jiangwan Road (Wikipedia); tell the driver 鲁迅公园. Chinese Wikipedia's 'Memorial Hall open every day' is out of date; it closes Mondays.

Old Yuyang Lane No. 2 (where the Party's first Shanghai group formed) and Huangpu's other hidden Party sites 中国共产党发起组成立地（《新青年》编辑部）旧址（老渔阳里2号）；又新印刷所旧址；中共中央政治局机关旧址（1928—1931年）

Small restored Party sites in Huangpu's 'red open-air museum' set · Nanchang Road, Fuxing Middle Road and Yunnan Middle Road, Huangpu District

Likely

This is the story before the First Congress. In spring 1920 Party founder Chen Duxiu moved the magazine New Youth into house 2 of Old Yuyang Lane, where Shanghai's first communist group formed that summer. At the Politburo site the Party's leaders worked underground from May 1928 until security chief Gu Shunzhang defected in late April 1931.

Metro
Printing House corner: at Xintiandi station (Lines 10 and 13). Old Yuyang Lane: about 0.7 km from Xintiandi or South Huangpi Road. Politburo site: nearest station not confirmed.
Hours
Weekly hours not found. Huangpu's notice set 09:00-16:30 for Wed 1 Jul 2026 at four sites. A 2021 report gave Wednesday as the Politburo site's closing day.
Tickets and booking
No price found: ask at the door. Notices say individuals need no reservation and are admitted in batches; groups register on the 上海黄浦 WeChat account or app.
Time needed
Old Yuyang Lane: 20 to 30 min (visits capped at 15 min, per a 2021 report). Politburo site: 40 to 60 min. Printing House corner: 5 min.
More: what to see, how to, tips and cautions
What to see
Old Yuyang Lane No. 2: a small restored shikumen with New Youth covers and an old desk. Politburo site: an immersive film, recreated offices, a courtyard mural. Youxin Printing House corner: what can be entered is unconfirmed.
How to
Show the driver 南昌路100弄2号 (Old Yuyang Lane) or 云南中路171-173号 (Politburo site). Look for the red-site plaques. Individuals need no booking.

Local tip

Watch out
Regular days and any price are unconfirmed. The Politburo site 'resumed opening' on 1 July 2026. The claim that Youxin Printing House printed the first full Chinese Communist Manifesto is disputed (Chengyu Lane is also named). Founding date: 'early August 1920' only.

Soong Ching-ling's Former Residence, Shanghai 上海宋庆龄故居纪念馆

House museum with garden, dovecote and garage · Huaihai Middle Road, former French Concession, Xuhui District

Confirmed

Soong Ching-ling, Sun Yat-sen's widow and later an honorary head of state of the People's Republic, lived here from spring 1949 (museum: 1949 to 1979, her Shanghai years). From this house she welcomed the liberation of Shanghai and donated her Stalin International Prize to found a maternity hospital. The main rooms are kept as she left them.

From the hotel
Too far to walk: about 5 km by road. Taxi 15 to 20 min; Line 10 from Xintiandi to Jiaotong University, 20 to 25 min in all (estimates).

Line 10 or 11, Jiaotong University station. Line 10 also stops at Xintiandi; confirm the number of stops in your metro app.

Tickets and booking
CNY 20 adult; CNY 10 students and 60+ (with ID); free for 70+, disabled visitors and military. Buy online via the museum's QR code; passport use not stated.
Time needed
About 1 to 1.5 hours with the garden (estimate).
More: what to see, how to, tips and cautions
What to see
A red-roofed Western house built in 1920, with her living room, bedroom and office as she left them; century-old camphor trees; a dovecote; a garage with a 1952 Soviet ZIS limousine and a 1970s Hongqi sedan.

Size guide. Chinese children's clothes are sized by the child's height in cm, not by age. Labels read height/chest for tops and dresses (for example 120/60) and height/waist for trousers (for example 120/53); baby labels may show months (for example 24M). Measure each child, then buy that number or the next size up and try it on, because brands run differently. To turn age into a rough starting size, these are the WHO median heights (boys/girls, cm), with the size to try after each arrow: 6 months 67.6/65.7; 12 months 75.7/74.0 → 80; 18 months 82.3/80.7 → 80 or 90; 2 years 87.1/85.7 → 90; 3 years 96.1/95.1 → 100; 4 years 103.3/102.7 → 100 or 110; 5 years 110.0/109.4 → 110; 6 years 116.0/115.1 → 120; 7 years 121.7/120.8 → 120 or 130; 8 years 127.3/126.6 → 130; 9 years 132.6/132.5 → 130 or 140; 10 years 137.8/138.6 → 140; 11 years 143.1/145.0 → 140 or 150; 12 years 149.1/151.2 → 150.
