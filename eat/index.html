About this list

How these were chosen

Of the 36 Chinese restaurants, 35 come from Dianping’s 2026 Must-Eat List, within about 10 km of People’s Square. Each of those 35 has ratings on Dianping and Amap and a second source of one of the five kinds explained below. For nine of them the second source is the Amap rating itself, which then counts twice, and the entry's note says “Second source: Amap”. Ratings read between 11 and 14 Nov 2026. The House of Rong was added from the Michelin Guide; its Dianping rating was read on 14 Nov 2026, and its Amap rating was read on 13 Nov 2026.

Picked for overall quality from Michelin, Gambero Rosso and named food writers (2024 to 2026). Dianping and Amap ratings were read between 11 and 14 Nov 2026; check the hours and book ahead.

Nightlife picks: picked for overall quality from named, dated reviews and lists (2024 to 2026). Where a dated event or a recent review was found it shows as “Evidence it is open”; where none shows, confirm the venue is trading before you go. These are music-led clubs and live venues; bottle-service clubs were reviewed and left out. Venues open, close and move often: check the venue’s own WeChat or Instagram for the night you plan.

How to read the second source

Michelin is listed in the Michelin Guide Shanghai (9 picks). Press / writer is original reporting or a first-hand visit by a named reporter or an established food writer (10). Crowd rating is 4.3 or more from at least 100 reviews on Trip.com or Ctrip for the same branch (4). Amap rating is 4.3 or more from at least 100 Amap reviews on the exact branch, a second source outside Meituan, checked by hand (9). Two diners is two separate people's meal write-ups on different sites, the weakest of the five (4).

Reviews, spend and distance

Dianping, Meituan and the Black Pearl guide share one owner, so none of them counts as a second source. Reviews are Dianping's own counts (Amap's are shown separately). Spend is the Dianping average per person for the 35 Dianping-list picks; added picks state their own price source. Links open the source in a new tab; most are in Chinese. "No closure found" is not proof a place is open, so check the hours.
