How to read the second source

Michelin is listed in the Michelin Guide Shanghai (9 picks). Press / writer is original reporting or a first-hand visit by a named reporter or an established food writer (10). Crowd rating is 4.3 or more from at least 100 reviews on Trip.com or Ctrip for the same branch (4). Amap rating is 4.3 or more from at least 100 Amap reviews on the exact branch, a second source outside Meituan, checked by hand (9). Two diners is two separate people's meal write-ups on different sites, the weakest of the five (4).

Reviews, spend and distance

Dianping, Meituan and the Black Pearl guide share one owner, so none of them counts as a second source. Reviews are Dianping's own counts (Amap's are shown separately). Spend is the Dianping average per person for the 35 Dianping-list picks; added picks state their own price source. Links open the source in a new tab; most are in Chinese. "No closure found" is not proof a place is open, so check the hours.
