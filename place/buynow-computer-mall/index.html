Buynow Computer Mall, Xujiahui (Bainaohui) 百脑汇（徐家汇）

General IT mall · Xuhui

CheckedSite and reports read · 15 Nov 2026

Laptops and phones are on the ground floor, PC builds on the third. Prices sit at multi-year highs.

Address

339 Caoxi Bei Lu (Caoxi North Road), near Nandan Dong Lu, Xuhui District. The mall's own website calls it 百脑汇上海店 (Buynow Shanghai store).

Hours
Official hours (Buynow's own store page, read 15 Nov 2026): 10:00 to 21:00 for the ground-floor IT mall and 10:00 to 22:00 for the basement restaurants. The upper floors (third-floor PC builders, fifth-floor repair stalls) are not listed, so shops may keep their own hours; go between 11:00 and 20:00. One fifth-floor repair stall lists 10:30 to 19:30 (SmartShanghai, March 2026). Mall phone: 021-31770606.
Price
At multi-year highs in 2026. August 2026 (IT时报, reporter on site): RTX 5060 graphics card about CNY 3,200, up from about 2,300 two months earlier; 16GB DDR5 memory about CNY 1,000, rising to nearly 1,500 within a month; Lenovo Legion Y9000P gaming laptop CNY 16,999 (about 12,000 at the start of 2026); ThinkBook 16 over CNY 10,000 (under 7,000 last year); Honor Magic 8 phone from CNY 3,899, but only after stacking national subsidy, student and shop promotions. January 2026 (The Paper), brand counters: ASUS from CNY 4,899 to 5,199; Lenovo about 6 to 7 percent up; Dell about 10 percent up; Huawei laptops about CNY 200 up. ASUS staff said brand counters follow manufacturer-set prices. Whether the DIY shops negotiate was not reported.
Metro
Xujiahui station (Lines 1, 9 and 11), about a 5 to 10 minute walk (OpenStreetMap puts it 0.28 km away in a straight line; SmartShanghai's listing says 10 minutes). From Huangpi South Road station on Line 1 it is 4 stops (South Shaanxi Road, Changshu Road, Hengshan Road, Xujiahui), about 10 minutes riding (estimate).
Area
Xujiahui, Xuhui District, about a 5 to 10 minute walk from Xujiahui metro

Why go

The only general IT mall that could be confirmed as trading in 2026, so it is the first stop for a visitor who wants an electronics mall. Reporters in January and August 2026 describe mainly brand-name laptop counters (ASUS, Lenovo, Dell, Huawei), a phone zone on the ground floor and PC-building counters on the third floor; SmartShanghai lists a MacBook repair stall on the fifth floor. It is best for a branded laptop or phone from an authorised counter, a custom PC build or a repair. Prices are at multi-year highs, so do not expect bargains, and no 2026 source describes accessory or gadget stalls or used goods.

How to get there

Show the driver: 请送我到 上海市徐汇区漕溪北路339号 百脑汇 (Please take me to Buynow, 339 Caoxi North Road). The August 2026 visit found the mall crowded with students and parents, so a weekday is probably easier. For a laptop or phone, go to the ground-floor brand counters. For a PC build, go to the third floor. Useful phrases: 可以用外国信用卡吗？(Can I use a foreign credit card?) 保修在国外有效吗？(Is the warranty valid abroad?) 能开发票吗？(Can you issue an invoice?)

Local tip

On the third floor, PC-build prices are rewritten on a blackboard and quotes are valid for that day only (IT时报, Aug 2026). Ask the shop to write every part's brand and model and the total (请把配置和型号写下来), confirm the price is for the same day, and check the same model's online price on your phone before you agree.

Watch out

(1) Prices are at multi-year highs and change daily, so get a written, same-day price with model numbers. (2) The mall is not at the station door: allow about 5 to 10 minutes' walk from Xujiahui metro. (3) No source says whether the national subsidy (国补), student discounts or other offers that staff mention are open to foreign visitors, so ask first. (4) Payment methods and foreign-card acceptance are unknown. Counters may prefer WeChat Pay or Alipay, so carry cash as a fallback. (5) A mainland-China warranty may not be honoured abroad, so ask for the terms in writing. (6) For phones, buy sealed units from the brand counters, because no 2026 source describes used or refurbished phone stalls here. (7) Do not give the driver 漕溪北路11号: an older 'Buy Now' listing at that address is marked closed. (8) Reputation: one November 2024 Ctrip travel blog says many online users call the mall unreliable (不靠谱) and suggests treating it as a sight to browse. The 2026 reports are price reports and describe a busy mall. (9) A January 2026 figure of about CNY 2,000 for 32GB DDR5 memory in The Paper's report came from a shop at the Qiujiang Road market, not from Buynow, so it is not a Buynow price.
