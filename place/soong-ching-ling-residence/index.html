Soong Ching-ling's Former Residence, Shanghai 上海宋庆龄故居纪念馆

House museum with garden, dovecote and garage · Xuhui

CheckedOfficial notices read · 15 Nov 2026

Rooms are kept as she left them, with a dovecote and old cars. Closed Mondays; closures are common.

Address

No. 1843 Huaihai Middle Road, Xuhui District

Hours
Tue-Sun 09:00-17:00, ticket sales stop 16:30; closed Mondays except public holidays (so Mon 30 Nov). Temporary-closure notices are posted often.
Tickets
CNY 20 adult; CNY 10 students and 60+ (with ID); free for 70+, disabled visitors and military. Buy online via the museum's QR code; passport use not stated.
Time
About 1 to 1.5 hours with the garden (estimate).
Best time
Weekday morning or Sunday morning, then lunch on Huaihai Middle Road. Not Monday; check the official notice for 28 and 29 Nov.
Metro
Line 10 or 11, Jiaotong University station. Line 10 also stops at Xintiandi; confirm the number of stops in your metro app.
Area
Huaihai Middle Road, former French Concession, Xuhui District

Why it matters

Soong Ching-ling, Sun Yat-sen's widow and later an honorary head of state of the People's Republic, lived here from spring 1949 (museum: 1949 to 1979, her Shanghai years). From this house she welcomed the liberation of Shanghai and donated her Stalin International Prize to found a maternity hospital. The main rooms are kept as she left them.

What to see

A red-roofed Western house built in 1920, with her living room, bedroom and office as she left them; century-old camphor trees; a dovecote; a garage with a 1952 Soviet ZIS limousine and a 1970s Hongqi sedan.

How to get there

Show: 请带我去淮海中路1843号宋庆龄故居. Buy the ticket on your phone via the museum's QR code, or ask your hotel front desk to do it; carry your passport.

Local tip

Look for the garage and dovecote in the garden: the museum itself advertises the cars and pigeons as 'living relics'.

Watch out

Temporary closures are frequent (notices on 9 Nov and 11 Jul 2026): check the official site the day before. Foreign-passport handling for the QR ticket is not stated; ask 021-64747183. First owner is disputed: American navigator Bauer (museum) or Leo R. Ball (Wikipedia).
