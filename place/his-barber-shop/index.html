His Barber Shop (Shaanxi Nan Road) 他的理发店

Vintage-themed men's barbershop · Xuhui

LikelyAmap reviews only

Expect vintage cuts in an old villa and the highest average spend of these barbers: CNY 394 on Amap.

Address

164 Shaanxi Nan Lu (Shaanxi South Road), at the corner of Changle Lu. Amap files it under Xuhui; SmartShanghai under Huangpu. The number 164 is the same in both.

Daily 11:00 to 21:00 (Amap, read 15 Nov 2026). SmartShanghai agrees, but its text dates from June 2020 and is marked 'Not verified'.
Price
Amap average spend CNY 394 (17 ratings, read 15 Nov 2026), the highest of the shops here. An Amap review (12 Nov 2025) says men's prices are around CNY 300, and a Weibo aggregator (11 Oct 2026, low reliability) says CNY 288 to 388. The only shave price found is CNY 128 from 2020, which is six years old. Ask the price first.
Metro
South Shaanxi Road (Lines 1, 10, 12), about a 5-minute walk (SmartShanghai). From Xintiandi station it is one stop west on Line 10, then walk (stop count not checked against a metro map).
Area
Xuhui/Huangpu border, Shaanxi Nan Lu at Changle Lu (Former French Concession)

Amap reviews only

The evidence for this one has a gap. Check on the day or call ahead.

Why go

Amap tags describe American-style and vintage haircuts and men's short hair, and reviewers say the work is good. One has cut his hair here for six years (28 Jul 2026), another says the staff are friendly with jazz and drinks (Aug 2025), and a third says a husband's cut turned out very handsome (16 Sep 2026). A 2020 SmartShanghai review reports precise beard lines and a very close straight-razor shave for CNY 128, but no hot towel. The evidence is Amap ratings plus that old review, so confidence is medium.

Independent or chain

Small local group, at least 2 shops. Amap also lists 'His Barbershop 他的理发馆·高定馆' at 巨鹿路741弄-6 (Jing'an Temple station exit 8, 400 m), average CNY 422. Group size beyond that was not checked.

How to get there

No online booking route found. Amap lists three mobile numbers: 13162887316, 15618594998 and 16621171445 (the first also appears on SmartShanghai). Ask hotel staff or a Chinese-speaking friend to phone or message them. Walking in during opening hours is possible but was not verified. Show the driver: 陕西南路164号 他的理发店. An Amap review says to look for the iron gate marked 164 at the corner and walk in.

Local tip

Look for the iron gate marked 164 at the corner of Shaanxi Nan Lu and Changle Lu. Ask the price, and whether a straight-razor shave is offered, before you sit.

About the ratings

Amap 4.8 (17 ratings), read 15 Nov 2026.

Watch out

Highest price of the shops here. English is not documented anywhere that was read. No 2026 source confirms that the straight-razor shave is still on the menu, so ask before sitting. SmartShanghai's own page is a June 2020 text marked 'Not verified' with no prices, and the CNY 128 shave price is from 2020. The Amap average (CNY 394) is above the CNY 288 to 388 and around-CNY 300 figures quoted elsewhere.

What was checked, and what was not

Read by 16 Nov 2026

The name, address (164 Shaanxi Nan Lu, near Changle Lu) and hours (11:00 to 21:00) are consistent across SmartShanghai (editor text 30 Jun 2020), the City8 directory (14 Aug 2023), Amap and Bing Maps (a hair-salon pin at the same address). The 2020 SmartShanghai review of barber Li (CNY 128, close shave, no hot towel) was checked and belongs to this shop, not to 595 Barbershop. A Weibo aggregator (11 Oct 2026, low reliability) describes a six-year-old villa shop at CNY 288 to 388. The 2026 operation rests on the Amap reviews of 16 Sep and 28 Jul 2026.

Not checked

That the shop is open in late 2026: this rests on Amap reviews alone, and no shop channel (Instagram, WeChat or Dianping) or other 2026 source could be read. English-language service. Whether the straight-razor shave is still offered, and any 2026 price. Walk-in policy. Card or wallet payment. A SmartShanghai list page appeared to show a different address for a shop of this name; it could not be opened and may be another business, so it was left out. How many shops the group has beyond two.
