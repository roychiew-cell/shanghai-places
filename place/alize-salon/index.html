Alize Salon & Barbershop (Yuyuan Road) 爱俪榭专业造型沙龙

Independent unisex salon · Jing’an

LikelyEnglish not tested · 15 Nov 2026

Haircuts run CNY 188 to 880 by stylist at this unisex salon. Ask for Super 8, said to speak English.

Address

English not tested

The evidence for this one has a gap. Check on the day or call ahead.

Why go

A salon open since 2006 (SmartShanghai). Its listing, edited 5 Aug 2026 and labelled 'Recently verified', names master barbers Harry, Justin, Super 8 and 格鲁 (Gelu). SmartShanghai says Super 8 communicates well in English, so ask for him when booking (not guaranteed every shift). Amap reviews dated Jul 2025 to Apr 2026 praise a stylist's technique and a polite, patient, professional team. It is a unisex salon with 151 listed services, not a craft barbershop, so it ranks last.

Independent or chain

Independent single salon (est. 2006); no second site found.

How to get there

Phone 139 0187 3840 (the same number on Amap and SmartShanghai); SmartShanghai lists WeChat, SMS or phone contact. Ask your hotel front desk or a Chinese-speaking friend to call or message to book a named master barber and confirm the price. A walk-in policy was not verified. Show the driver: 愚园路272号 爱俪榭专业造型沙龙, and the Amap pin 'AlizeSalon Barbershop' (it prints 愚园路270号).

Local tip

Name a master barber and confirm the price for that barber before you sit. Decline add-on treatments.

About the ratings

Amap 4.6 (5 ratings), read 15 Nov 2026.

Watch out

Door number: SmartShanghai prints 272 and Amap prints 270. A sister SmartShanghai entry at 270 Yuyuan Lu is marked closed but shares the same phone number, so the two numbers appear to be one frontage. Show the driver both. Prices swing from CNY 188 to 880 with stylist level, and the salon sells treatments above CNY 2,000, so decline add-ons. English is not guaranteed on every shift. A 2020 SmartShanghai review says the shave was close but left red bumps for about two hours. The barber-specific craft evidence is weaker than for Doc's.
