Zhapu Road Bridge: the photographers' bridge over Suzhou Creek 乍浦路桥（又称二摆渡桥）

Open-air viewpoint on a historic road bridge · Huangpu

CheckedPress reports read

Look east to Waibaidu Bridge and the Lujiazui towers for free. Arrive about 16:40; sunset is 16:52.

Address

Zhapu Road Bridge over Suzhou Creek, joining Huqiu Road (south bank) and Zhapu Road (north bank); 248 m upstream of Waibaidu Bridge. Also called Er Baidu Qiao, 'Second Ferry Bridge'.

Hours
A public road bridge with pavements: no gate, no hours. 28 Nov: sunrise 06:32, sunset 16:52, civil dusk 17:18 (calculated and checked).
Tickets
Free. No reservation.
Time
20-40 minutes on the bridge; add 30-45 minutes for the Bund and Waibaidu Bridge.
Best time
Blue hour: arrive about 16:40, sunset 16:52, stay to about 17:30 as Lujiazui lights up. Sunrise (06:32) is the other classic moment. Cold wind: bring a coat.
Metro
Nanjing East Road (Lines 2, 10) about 1 km south, or Tiantong Road (Lines 10, 12) about 0.7-1 km north-west; distances not map-checked.
Area
Huangpu / Hongkou border, Suzhou Creek, beside Rockbund (Waitanyuan, 外滩源)

Why it matters

From mid-bridge you look east along Suzhou Creek to Waibaidu Bridge (the steel truss usually billed as China's first, completed Dec 1907 and opened Jan 1908) and the Lujiazui towers. The concrete Zhapu Road Bridge dates from 1927 (earlier: a missionary's floating bridge, then a wooden one in the 1870s) and was listed as an excellent historic building in 1994. Locals come for views, exercise and wedding photos.

What to see

Looking east: Waibaidu Bridge close up, Broadway Mansions (上海大厦, 1934) on the north bank, and the Lujiazui towers beyond, reflected in the creek at dusk. Photographers rest cameras on the railing.

How to get there

Taxi to 乍浦路桥 (or get out at Huqiu Road). Stand on the pavement mid-bridge and look east; Waibaidu Bridge is 250 m east.

Local tip

Do the bridge first at 16:40, then walk to the Bund for the lights. Sakura Valley rest station (樱花谷驿站, 186 South Suzhou Road; hours unknown) offers rest and phone charging.

Watch out

It is a working road bridge: stay on the pavement, no tripods in the walkway. Photographers crowd the best railing spots at sunrise and sunset; arrive early or stand a few metres along. Crowded at peak times.
