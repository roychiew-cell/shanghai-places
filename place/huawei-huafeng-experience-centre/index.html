Shanghai Huafeng Experience Centre (Huawei intelligent collaboration showroom, dealer-run) 上海华峰体验中心

Dealer-run Huawei showroom · Minhang

CheckedOfficial page read · 17 Nov 2026

Dealer-run Huawei showroom for IdeaHub meeting screens; Mon to Fri 9:00 to 17:30; book first.

Address

Room 405, Building 26, Lane 99, Chunguang Road, Minhang District, Shanghai

Monday to Friday 9:00 to 17:30 (Huawei's page prints 周一至周五9：00-17：30); weekends are not listed. Source: Huawei Enterprise page for 上海华峰体验中心, read 17 Nov 2026.
Price
not published
Metro
No station, line or walking time is given by Huawei's page. OpenStreetMap puts Xinzhuang station about 3.75 km from the lane in a straight line and Waihuanlu station about 4.85 km (the building itself is not mapped), so a taxi ride is the practical way.
Area
Minhang District, Chunguang Road Lane 99, Xinzhuang Industrial Zone (OpenStreetMap), outer south-west Shanghai; nearest metro Xinzhuang, about 3.75 km in a straight line (OpenStreetMap); no source states a walking time

Why go

Huawei's own page lists it as the Shanghai entry among its authorised-dealer experience centres and lists the IdeaHub B3, IdeaHub Board 3 Pro, IdeaHub B2 Base and IdeaHub S2 under it. It is the only Shanghai entry on Huawei's list of such centres.

How to get there

Book first: the page offers 预约参观 (book a visit), 现场讲解 (on-site explanation), 下单购买 (order) and 送货上门 (delivery), with a 联系门店 (contact the store) button, and lists the phones 18918969959 and 64684646. Ask your hotel front desk or a Chinese-speaking friend to phone. The address is Room 405 in Building 26 of a lane, so it is an office suite, not a shop front.

Local tip

Huawei's enterprise hotline, 400-822-9999 (Huawei Enterprise collaboration page, read 17 Nov 2026), can be used to ask which models are on the floor, because the centre page ends its model list with 等 ('and others').

Watch out

The centre is run by a dealer, 上海华峰办公设备有限公司, not by Huawei itself. Weekend opening, walk-in rules, prices and stock are not stated. It is far from the city centre and from any metro station, and the page content is undated.

What was checked, and what was not

Read on 17 Nov 2026

Read on 17 Nov 2026. Huawei Enterprise's 智能协作体验中心查询 page holds one list entry for 上海市 (listed as 上海华为智能协作展厅), and the centre's own page (title 上海华峰体验中心, header time stamp 16 Nov 2026) gives the operator 上海华峰办公设备有限公司, the address, hours, two phone numbers and the IdeaHub models named above. The page description says the centre is run by a Huawei-authorised dealer and offers sales and after-sales for IdeaHub and CloudLink. OpenStreetMap maps Chunguang Road Lane 99 in the Xinzhuang Industrial Zone.

Not checked

Weekend opening, whether walk-ins are admitted, prices and stock, and the room and building entrance (OpenStreetMap shows the lane but not Building 26). No second independent source for the address was found. Whether the dealer keeps its entry current is not known.
