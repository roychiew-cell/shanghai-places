Bicester Village Shanghai (called 'Shanghai Village' on its own site) 比斯特上海购物村

Outlet village (brand outlets) · Pudong

CheckedOfficial source read · 15 Nov 2026

This outlet village for imported menswear is far out in Pudong, so plan half a day.

Address

88 Shendi Road (East), Pudong New Area (navigation address '88 Shendi Rd (E)' on the village's own page)

Open in Amap (opens Amap, or its website, in a new tab)

See on the map

Drop a pin in Amap instead (opens Amap, or its website, in a new tab)

Hours
Mon-Thu 10:00-22:00; Fri-Sun and public holidays 09:30-22:00 (Shanghai government listing updated 24 Oct 2025, sourced from the village's WeChat; repeated by TravelOfChina, 26 Oct 2026). An older government listing (Dec 2023) showed earlier closing, at 21:00 and 21:30. Individual shops can close earlier; the village's own web page hides the hours, so check the Bicester Life mini-program.
Price
Outlet discounts. TravelOfChina lists shopper-recorded ranges (undated; for example Tommy Hilfiger 30 to 70 percent) and says current-season icons at major houses usually run at full price. No 2026 price was verified. Tax refund (the village's own page): shown as 9 percent; minimum CNY 200 per store per day; instant refund at the welcome centre (named 'Millay Welcome Center' on the village's page and 'Guest Welcome Center' in a Dec 2025 government article), and some brands refund in the shop. You must leave Shanghai within 28 days, bring your passport and a credit card that can be pre-authorised, and keep goods unopened and unused for the customs check at the airport.
Metro
Line 11 to Disney Resort station, Exit 4, then resort shuttle bus 1 (国际旅游度假区1路), about CNY 1 and about 10 minutes (TravelOfChina, 26 Oct 2026); not walkable. Line 10 three stops from Xintiandi station to Jiaotong University, change to Line 11 to Disney Resort (not timed; likely over an hour with the shuttle). By taxi the village's own page gives about 45 minutes from downtown.
Area
Pudong, Shanghai International Resort (next to Shanghai Disney)

Why go

This is the best-evidenced outlet for imported menswear. The village's official brand directory lists, among others, Zegna, Armani, BOSS, Canali, Kiton, Brunello Cucinelli, Tom Ford, Dunhill, Bally, Brooks Brothers, Polo Ralph Lauren, Thom Browne, Burberry, Barbour, Canada Goose, Loro Piana and Bosideng. The directory does not show which units are trading. It is operating in 2026: its own site carries a 2026 copyright and a live brand directory, and Winshang (9 May 2026) reported strong May Day results.

How to get there

Take a taxi or Didi to 申迪东路88号 (比斯特上海购物村), or use the metro plus shuttle. Plan a half-day trip. The village's own page says it is about 20 minutes by car from Pudong airport, so it could be a stop on the way to or from the airport. No luggage-storage information was found for Bicester.

Local tip

Do not plan two outlets in one day (Trip.com guide).

Watch out

Opening hours rest on one government listing and TravelOfChina, so check on the day. Payment accepted per TravelOfChina: WeChat Pay, Alipay, bank cards, foreign cards and digital yuan (e-CNY) (its 92 percent foreign-card figure is from Sep 2023). No Black Friday or Singles' Day programme for 2026 was found. A winter market and year-end offers ran in Dec 2025 (blind box through 4 Jan); nothing is announced for 2026 yet. Brand presence in a directory is not proof of stock or sizes.

What was checked, and what was not

What was read

Read on 14 and 15 Nov 2026 from several sources. The address matches the village's own page and the Shanghai government listing. It is trading in 2026: the village's own site (2026 copyright, live brand directory and tax-refund page, read 15 Nov 2026), Winshang (9 May 2026, May Day trading) and TravelOfChina ('last verified 23 Oct 2026'). The Line 11 plus resort shuttle route is given by TravelOfChina (26 Oct 2026) and, independently, by Trip.com's outlet guide. The brand list was read directly from the official directory. The tax-refund rules come from the village's own page and the CNY 200 minimum matches Xinhua (2 Jul 2026).

Not checked

Hours rest on one Shanghai government listing (updated 24 Oct 2025, sourced from the village's WeChat) repeated by TravelOfChina; the village's own web page loads its hours dynamically and could not be read. Which brands are trading and which sizes are in stock. Any 2026 Black Friday or winter-market programme (none found). Prices: no 2026 price was read. Luggage storage at Bicester (none found). Shuttle buses from Xujiahui (CNY 20) and Pudong IFC Mall (CNY 15) were mentioned in 2024 and were not confirmed for 2026. The 45-minute drive is the village's own figure. No Dianping or Amap ratings were read.
