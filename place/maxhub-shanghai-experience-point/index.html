MAXHUB Shanghai official experience point MAXHUB（上海）官方体验点

Brand experience point · Xuhui

CheckedOfficial page read · 17 Nov 2026

Brand-run MAXHUB experience point on an office floor in Xuhui; book first by form or hotline.

Address

25th floor, Building 19, No. 1515 Gumei Road, Xuhui District, Shanghai

Open in Amap (opens Amap, or its website, in a new tab)

See on the map

Hours
Not stated for the experience point itself. MAXHUB's contact page (read 17 Nov 2026) lists its Shanghai office at the same address, the 25th floor of Building 19, with hours 9:00 - 18:00 and the national hotline 400-168-9545, which the locator page calls a 24-hour line; the days of the week are not stated.
Price
not published
Metro
Nearest station in OpenStreetMap: 虹梅路 (Hongmei Road), about 0.33 km in a straight line from the Xuhui section of Gumei Road; then 东兰路 (Donglan Road) 0.99 km and 虹漕路 (Hongcao Road) 1.28 km. Building 19 at No. 1515 is not mapped, so the real distance is unknown, and no source gives a walking time.
Area
Xuhui District, Gumei Road, Hongmei Road subdistrict (OpenStreetMap); Hongmei Road station is about 0.33 km from the Xuhui section of Gumei Road in OpenStreetMap, but no source states a walking time

Why go

MAXHUB's own locator lists it under its meeting-solutions offline experience points as the brand's official Shanghai point, and the page carries a visit-booking form. It is the brand's own entry, not a partner's.

How to get there

By booking. The locator's pop-up has a 预约访问 (book a visit) button, and the page has a 预约来访 form asking for name, phone, an optional email and a purpose (the first option is 了解产品, 'learn about the products'); the hotline is 400-168-9545. The address is the 25th floor of Building 19, so it is an office floor, not a shop front. Ask your hotel front desk or a Chinese-speaking friend to phone and book.

Local tip

The brand's site header links to MAXHUB's own online store and to JD and Tmall stores (官方商城, 京东, 天猫), so buying can be done online rather than on the floor; nothing on the page says units are sold at the experience point.

Watch out

The opening days, the products on display and whether walk-ins are admitted are not stated. The locator's map position is rounded to two decimals, which can put it about a kilometre out, so use the written address. The locator also lists two Shanghai partner offices, which are not described as showrooms.

What was checked, and what was not

Read on 17 Nov 2026

Read on 17 Nov 2026. MAXHUB China's service-network page (footer 2026, operator 广州视臻信息科技有限公司) holds a list headed MAXHUB会议解决方案 线下体验点 (offline experience points for its meeting solutions); its embedded list has three Shanghai entries, and 'MAXHUB（上海）官方体验点' is the brand's own. The page also carries the visit-booking form, the 24-hour hotline and a product menu naming meeting tablets, LED all-in-ones, video-conferencing terminals, cameras, microphones and audio processing. MAXHUB's contact page lists a Shanghai office at the same address, with hours 9:00 - 18:00. OpenStreetMap maps the Xuhui section of Gumei Road and Hongmei Road station.

Not checked

Which days the point is open (the Shanghai office is listed 9:00 - 18:00), which products are on display, whether walk-ins are admitted, and the address in a second independent place (OpenStreetMap shows Gumei Road in Xuhui but not No. 1515). The page is undated apart from a 2026 copyright line.
