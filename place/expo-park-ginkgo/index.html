Expo Park ginkgo grove 世博公园

Riverside ginkgo grove · Pudong

LikelySome facts open · 27 Nov 2026

Ginkgo groves beside the river.

Address

1750 Shibo Avenue, Expo Park, Pudong

Open in Amap (opens Amap, or its website, in a new tab)

See on the map

Approximate park pin, rather than a precise grove or entrance pin.

Hours
For Sat 28, Sun 29 and Mon 30 Nov, aim for 09:00 to 17:00. Published hours disagree: the 2021 city list and Amap say all day; other listings give 09:00 to 19:00 or 09:00 to 17:00. This is a suggested visiting window, not confirmed opening hours.
Price
Free entry, reported on 27 Nov 2026.
Best time
Daylight.
Metro
Nearest station: Shibo Avenue (世博大道, Line 13), about 500 m in a straight line to the park pin.
Area
Pudong

Some facts open

There is one good source, or the sources disagree on a detail. Check on the day or call ahead.

Why it matters

A 27 Nov report says the leaves are at their best.

What to see

Look for the “Ginkgo Gate” (银杏之门) photo spot and a covered bridge, named in the 27 Nov report. Official descriptions also place a grove east of the large lawn.

How to get there

From Shibo Avenue station (Line 13), head towards the park on Shibo Avenue. A 19 Nov guide suggests Gate 7, then the path on the right into the autumn garden. China Art Museum (Line 8) is another approach named in the 27 Nov report, useful if you are already on Line 8.

What was checked, and what was not

Checked 27 Nov 2026, updated 28 Nov: the correct park, official grove description and published seasonal report.

Not checked

The 27 Nov report gives no observation time or photographs. Leaf colour on 28 Nov, gate access and the precise route between the autumn garden and the grove are not confirmed.
