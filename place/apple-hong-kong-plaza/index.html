Address

In English

Apple Hong Kong Plaza: 282 Huaihai Middle Road, North Tower, Huangpu District. Alternatives: Apple iapm, 999 Huaihai Middle Road (inside iapm mall), Xuhui District; Apple Nanjing East Road, 300 East Nanjing Road, Huangpu District; Huawei flagship, 233 East Nanjing Road, Huangpu District.

Hours
Apple Hong Kong Plaza, Apple iapm and Apple Nanjing East Road: 10:00 to 22:00 daily for 15 to 21 Nov 2026 (Apple's pages). Hours for later weeks are not yet posted, so check Apple's page the week before. Huawei flagship: Mon to Sun 10:00 to 22:00 (Huawei's own page, read 15 Nov 2026).

Hong Kong Plaza: Huangpi South Road station (Line 1), 130 m; Xintiandi station (Lines 10 and 13) is about 0.4 km away. iapm: South Shaanxi Road station (Lines 1, 10 and 12), about 0.1 km; it is one stop from Xintiandi station on Line 10 toward Shanghai Library. Apple Nanjing East Road and Huawei: East Nanjing Road station (Lines 2 and 10), three stops from Xintiandi station on Line 10 (Laoximen, Yuyuan Garden, East Nanjing Road).
Area
Huaihai Middle Road, Huangpu District (Hong Kong Plaza); alternatives in Xuhui (iapm) and on East Nanjing Road, Huangpu

Why go

This is the official route for iPhones, Macs and Apple support: manufacturer pricing, a Genius Bar by appointment, same-day pickup of online orders and trade-in (Apple's store page). Apple Hong Kong Plaza (香港广场) is at 282 Huaihai Middle Road; Apple iapm is at 999 Huaihai Middle Road, Xuhui. Huawei's flagship at 233 East Nanjing Road is a few minutes' walk from Apple's Nanjing East Road store, and a news report of 2 Oct 2025 found both open with shoppers. In the same report new launches, such as the iPhone 17 series and Huawei's tri-fold phone, were out of stock.

How to get there

Book a Genius Bar slot in advance if you need support. For a purchase, check stock on Apple's site or app before going; Apple's page says online orders can be picked up in store the same day. For iapm: 请送我到 淮海中路999号 环贸iapm (Please take me to iapm, 999 Huaihai Middle Road). For Apple Nanjing East Road and Huawei, show the Chinese addresses below. Store phones: Hong Kong Plaza 400-617-1312; Huawei flagship 400-608-4456.

Local tip

Huawei's flagship (No. 233) and Apple's Nanjing East Road store (No. 300) are a few minutes' walk apart on the same street, so one trip covers both.

Watch out

(1) Hong Kong Plaza has an Apple store; iapm is another. (2) Only Apple's own pages cover Hong Kong Plaza and iapm. The independent news report (2 Oct 2025) covers the Nanjing East Road stores. (3) New iPhones and Huawei launches were out of stock in that report. Stock in Nov 2026 was not checked, so check Apple's site or app first. (4) Whether foreign cards are accepted is not stated on Apple's pages. (5) A mainland-China purchase may carry different warranty terms abroad. This was not verified, so ask. (6) Apple's hours for later weeks are not yet posted.
