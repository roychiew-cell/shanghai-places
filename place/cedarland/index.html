Good to know

Heatwolves praises the new C-Park club's surround sound and design in June, and its programming in November. An artist-issued notice identifies unit LG13 to 15; 658 Zhaohua Road is the complex reference.

Known for

Surround sound, multiple rooms and experimental live sets; city-pop and boogie also feature.

Before you go

658 Zhaohua Road is the complex's reference address; the street gate is unverified. Give the driver C-Park and LG13 to 15; check ticket and door rules, as foreign-passport ticketing and entry are unverified.

Watch out

Use the C-Park club, separate from the older Cedar Kitchen on Julu Road.
