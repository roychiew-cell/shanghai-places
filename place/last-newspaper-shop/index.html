Shanghai's last newspaper shop, Zhapu Road (moved there 1 Jan 2026) 上海最后一家报刊门市部（乍浦路430号）

Newspaper and magazine shop · Hongkou

LikelyLatest report Mar 2026

One man has run Shanghai's last newspaper shop since 1988. No report found after March 2026.

Address

430 Zhapu Road near Wujin Road, Hongkou District (about 30 sq m); Victory Cinema stands at 408 Zhapu Road, at the Haining Road corner, about 200 m away

Hours
Reported 06:00-18:00 daily with no rest day (The Paper, visit of 6 Jan 2026); Jiefang Daily says it closes at 17:30. Arrive before 17:00; daylight ends about 16:50.
Tickets
Free to browse. Newspapers cost a few yuan; magazines up to hundreds of yuan; about 1,000 titles. Payment method not reported (carry some cash).

Morning for the freshest dailies (opens about 06:00). Weekends bring younger visitors. Go before 16:00; daylight ends about 16:50.
Metro
Line 10 Sichuan North Road, Exit 2, about 5 min walk (SmartShanghai listing).
Area
Hongkou, Zhapu Road film-and-food street, near the north Bund

Latest report Mar 2026

The evidence for this one has a gap. Check on the day or call ahead.

Why it matters

Jiang Jun, now 67, has sold newspapers since 1988; thousands of Shanghai newsstands closed after 2010 and this is the last. After a public outcry over a planned closure, the street's operator offered rent-free space and it reopened on 1 January 2026. Regulars buy each paper for one favourite column.

What to see

A shop of about 30 sq m crammed with about 1,000 newspapers and magazines: dailies, film, history, geography and military titles. Regulars swap gossip at the counter and the owner hands each their usual paper.

How to get there

Line 10 to Sichuan North Road Exit 2, then walk about 5 min; or show 乍浦路430号 to a taxi. One English article misprints No. 189: ignore it.

Local tip

Buy a Chinese newspaper or a film or history magazine as a souvenir. Victory Cinema is about 200 m away (see its own card).

Watch out

Media attention swamped the owner in January 2026: buy something, skip interviews and long photo sessions, ask before photographing him or customers. He says he does not know how long he can continue; a hand-over is possible. Check Amap first.
