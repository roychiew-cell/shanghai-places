Good to know

The CNY 688 per-person set is a straightforward choice for a group that wants a fixed menu.

What recent diners say

Recent diners praised the service and room, but a 15 November diner criticised small portions and added charges. None of the first reviews read discussed the autumn crab set. The monthly overall score dipped to 3.9 in August and September and recovered to 4.2 in October. Read on Dianping on 21 Nov 2026.

Planning your visit

Reserve seven places through the venue restaurant page or call the published hotel number. Ask for the 2026 秋蟹雅宴 set; hotel lists 104 seats and 3 private rooms, but availability is unconfirmed.

Local tip

The official set includes a whole steamed crab and a hand-picked crab soup bun. Staff can pick the whole crab, according to the venue post.

Watch out

Offer is dated 15 Oct–31 Dec 2026, but stock and seats for a chosen evening need confirmation. The venue site does not guarantee a seven-person table.
