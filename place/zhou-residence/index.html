Zhou Residence: the Communist delegation's Shanghai office (Zhou Enlai, 1946) 周公馆（中国共产党代表团驻沪办事处旧址／纪念馆）

Historic villa museum with a garden · Huangpu

LikelyUndated official page · 15 Nov 2026

Zhou Enlai worked in this garden villa in 1946. Free; the house shuts at 17:00, the garden at 19:00.

Nos. 71 and 73 Sinan Road, Huangpu District (No. 107 Masinan Road in 1946)

Why it matters

In 1946, as Nationalist-Communist peace talks collapsed towards civil war, the Communist delegation rented this garden villa as its Shanghai office, and Zhou Enlai lived and worked here. Because the Nationalist authorities obstructed the office, it was registered as 'General Zhou Enlai's residence'. It shows how the Communist Party worked openly, under watch.

What to see

A three-storey 1920s villa: Zhou's office-bedroom, Dong Biwu's family room, 30-plus artefacts added in 2024, and the exhibition 'Zhou Enlai in Shanghai' in No. 71. The front garden opened to the public in July 2024.

How to get there

Show: 请带我去思南路73号周公馆. It is a 5- to 10-minute walk from Sun Yat-sen's house, so do both together. Carry your passport for the ID check.

Local tip

The garden is the one regular evening option among these sites (open to 19:00 until 31 March). The 1920s villas and cafes of Sinan Mansions surround it.

Watch out

A Trip.com listing reportedly shows it as temporarily not operating (unconfirmed, likely stale); no closure notice was found, so check the Memorial's WeChat account or have hotel staff or a Chinese-speaking friend phone 021-64730420 (official service guide). Older listings saying 'reservation required' or 'closed for renovation' are out of date. SmartShanghai calls the interpretation strongly Party-slanted.
