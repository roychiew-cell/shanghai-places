Rockbund and the Rockbund Art Museum 外滩源（洛克·外滩源）与上海外滩美术馆

Restored riverside block with a free art museum · Huangpu

CheckedOfficial site read · 18 Nov 2026

Add it to the Bund: a free art museum in a 1930s building on a restored block. Open Wed-Sun.

Address

20 Huqiu Road (Rockbund Art Museum), Rockbund, Huangpu District, Shanghai; near Yuanmingyuan Road

Hours
Museum: Wed-Sun 11:00-18:00, last admission 17:30. Tue 24 Nov is a members' day; Mon 30 Nov is not listed, so treat it as closed. The street and church exterior are open at all hours.
Tickets
Free since 2 May 2025. Wed-Fri you walk in with no booking. At weekends and on public holidays entry is still free, with an optional time slot on the museum's WeChat mini-program for priority entry, or you can queue. No passport or ID rule is stated.
Time
About 45 minutes outside plus 45 to 60 inside the museum (estimate); an add-on to the Bund, not a trip of its own.
Best time
Wed-Fri, with no weekend queue; a dry day helps for the street.
Metro
East Nanjing Road (南京东路), Lines 2 and 10, Exit 6 on the Henan Middle Road side near Tianjin Road; the museum gives about ten minutes on foot from there.
Area
Huangpu District, Waitanyuan (外滩源), north end of the Bund by Suzhou Creek

Why it matters

The Bund begins here, on the headland where Suzhou Creek meets the Huangpu, in one of Shanghai's first foreign settlements. The block holds the Union Church of 1886 and the Royal Asiatic Society building of 1932-33, by Palmer & Turner, now the art museum. David Chipperfield Architects restored it, and the Shanghai government says it reopened in autumn 2023.

What to see

The Rockbund Art Museum, in the Royal Asiatic Society building itself, is the main old interior you can enter free; its season runs to 21 Feb 2027, including Jota Mombaça's ‘stuck in movement (in the tide)’, a sound opera with no fixed stage and no live performers. Outside, Yuanmingyuan Road is a pedestrian street of restored buildings, now shops, restaurants and offices, with the Gothic Revival Union Church (新天安堂) near its creek end.

How to get there

Show: 请带我去上海外滩美术馆，虎丘路20号。 The main entrance is at the Museum Plaza, just east of Huqiu Road. Yuanmingyuan Road is pedestrian; give the driver Huqiu Road, where the museum is.

Local tip

Do the museum first, when it opens at 11:00 on a Wed-Fri, then walk Yuanmingyuan Road to the Union Church and on towards the river mouth and the Bund.

Watch out

The shops, cafes and restaurants are open to anyone, but the Lyceum Building (兰心大楼, listed as a private club) and the Capitol and Missions buildings (光陆大楼, 协进大楼, listed as offices) are facades only. Holy Trinity Church (圣三一堂) on Jiujiang Road is a different site, listed as off-limits.
