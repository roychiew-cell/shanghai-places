Lu Xun's last home (Dalu New Village No. 9) and Lu Xun Park 上海鲁迅故居（山阴路132弄9号，大陆新村9号）· 鲁迅公园（鲁迅墓、上海鲁迅纪念馆）

Preserved writer's house, plus a park · Hongkou

CheckedHours read, fee unsure · 18 Nov 2026

Look for the clock stopped at his death in 1936; his tomb is in Lu Xun Park. House closed Mondays.

Address

In English

House: No. 9, Lane 132, Shanyin Road, Hongkou. Lu Xun Park: 2288 North Sichuan Road (government page) or 146 East Jiangwan Road (Wikipedia). Memorial Hall inside the park: 200 Tianai Road.

Hours
House: 09:00-16:00, closed Mondays (Shanghai government, 31 Aug 2026). Memorial Hall: closed since 3 Nov 2025 for overall renovation, with no reopening date (Hongkou government notice of 29 Sep 2025). Park: 05:00-21:00 (Nov-Apr).
Tickets
House: CNY 8 on older listings, unconfirmed for 2026; entry in small timed groups. Park and Plum Garden free; Meixuan CNY 15. Memorial Hall: closed (see Hours).
Time
House: 30 to 40 minutes. Park, tomb and Memorial Hall: 1 to 1.5 hours (estimates).
Best time
Weekday morning. If you add Duolun Road (about 1 km south of the park), note the section between Sichuan North Road and Lane 201 is closed to vehicles 11:00-18:00 on Sat 28 and Sun 29 Nov (authorised vehicles excepted): ask the taxi to stop on Sichuan North Road. Taxis to the park and the house are not affected.
Metro
Lines 3 and 8, Hongkou Football Stadium, for the park. The house on Shanyin Road is about 0.7 km from the park (estimate).
Area
Hongkou District, north of Suzhou Creek

Why it matters

Lu Xun (1881-1936), founder of modern Chinese vernacular literature, spent his last nine years in Shanghai writing essays against the Nationalist censors and co-founded the League of Left-Wing Writers in 1930. He lived in this rented house from 11 April 1933 and died here on 19 October 1936. His tomb in the park carries Mao Zedong's calligraphy.

What to see

House: original furniture and his second-floor study-bedroom, with a calendar and clock stopped at his death. Park: his tomb, the Memorial Hall, the Plum Garden (free to stroll) and the Meixuan building's exhibition on Yun Bong-gil (CNY 15).

How to get there

Show: 请带我去山阴路132弄9号鲁迅故居 (house) or 鲁迅公园 (park). Taxi is easier than the metro. The house is small; ask your hotel to phone ahead at weekends (2020 advice).

Local tip

On 29 April 1932 Korean independence fighter Yun Bong-gil threw a bomb at a Japanese military celebration in this park (then Hongkew Park); the Meixuan building commemorates him.

Watch out

Park address differs by source: 2288 North Sichuan Road (Shanghai government, 2026) or 146 East Jiangwan Road (Wikipedia); tell the driver 鲁迅公园. Chinese Wikipedia's 'Memorial Hall open every day' is out of date: the hall has been shut for renovation since 3 Nov 2025.
