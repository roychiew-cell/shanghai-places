Yuyuan Road 愚园路

Suggested by JL

Historic shopping street · Jing’an

LikelySome facts open · 21 Nov 2026

A long residential street with early twentieth-century houses, lanes and newer small shops.

Address

Yuyuan Road, Changde Road to Changning Road, Jing’an and Changning districts

Some facts open

There is one good source, or the sources disagree on a detail. Check on the day or call ahead.

Why it matters

The protected historic block covers only part of the road, so allow time to choose a section.

What to see

Yuyuan Road Historical and Cultural Block; historic houses and lanes (Shanghai government, 2025). Yuyuan Department Store (official tourism site, 2023).

How to get there

Start near Jing’an Temple or Jiangsu Road and walk west; the full road is about 2.8 km.

Local tip

Do not confuse 愚园路 with Yu Garden 豫园, which is elsewhere.

Watch out

Residential lanes remain lived in; keep to public paths.
