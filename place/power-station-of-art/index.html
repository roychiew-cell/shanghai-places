Power Station of Art (contemporary art in a former power plant) 上海当代艺术博物馆（PSA）

Contemporary art museum · Huangpu

CheckedInformation reviewed · 18 Nov 2026

A former power plant shows contemporary art, 11:00-19:00. Closed Mondays; the current show is CNY 60.

Address

No. 678 Miaojiang Road, Huangpu District (older listings give 200 Huayuangang Road for the same site).

Open in Amap (opens Amap, or its website, in a new tab)

Hours
Tue-Sun 11:00-19:00, last entry 18:00. Closed Monday (so Mon 30 Nov); open on national holidays.
Tickets
Special shows are ticketed: buy at the 1F desk or the official WeChat service account. The current show, Liu Jiakun's 'The Moon and the Well' (14 Nov 2026 to 28 Feb 2027), is CNY 60 (city tourism portal, 28 Oct 2026). ID requirement not confirmed; carry your passport.
Time
1.5 to 2.5 hours; the Liu Jiakun show alone is worth about an hour.
Best time
A weekday afternoon (Tuesday to Friday), when it is quieter. Last entry 18:00 suits the dusk hours, when Yu Garden has closed. The galleries are kept cool: bring a layer.
Metro
Line 4 or 8, Xizang South Road station, Exit 2, then about 15 min on foot. No on-site parking.
Area
Huangpu, south Huangpu riverfront next to the former Expo site

Why it matters

It began as the Nanshi Electric Plant, founded by the Qing authorities in 1897 near the Shiliupu wharf. A new plant went up on this riverside site from 1935; the present building and 165 m chimney were completed in 1985, and the plant closed in 2007. It opened in 2012 as mainland China's first public contemporary art museum.

What to see

'Liu Jiakun: The Moon and the Well' (刘家琨：明月构想), the world's first major solo show of the 2025 Pritzker Prize winner, on the 7th floor and terrace, 14 Nov 2026 to 28 Feb 2027. Two other solo shows run too.

How to get there

Taxi to 上海当代艺术博物馆 (Miaojiang Road 678). Buy a ticket at the 1F desk or on the WeChat service account; show your passport if asked.

Local tip

The show's Chinese title is the name of Liu Jiakun's 1990s novel about an ideal city. Pair the visit with a taxi along the riverside to the West Bund.

Watch out

Closed Mondays. The museum's website is powerstationofart.com. Exhibition texts online are mostly Chinese; English wall texts not checked.
