Why go

The nearest place to Xintiandi metro station for drones and other DJI gear, about 0.6 to 0.8 km away. DJI's own page describes a two-storey store of about 600 m2 with a 20 m2 indoor flight area behind a large window, and a Swift Service Center for enquiries, system upgrades, troubleshooting, repairs and replacements. It is a brand showroom, not an IT mall, so treat it as a short supplement. Design write-ups from 2022 describe it as Shanghai's first DJI and Hasselblad concept flagship, in a building that keeps its traditional shikumen facade.

How to get there

Walk from Xintiandi metro station. If you take a taxi, show the driver: 请送我到 新天地 马当路119号 大疆旗舰店 (Please take me to the DJI flagship store at Xintiandi, 119 Madang Road). To avoid a wasted trip for a specific model, ask your hotel front desk or a Chinese-speaking friend to phone first: DJI's page lists 021-5388 2588 and 15618999721. DJI's page mentions a 20 m2 indoor flight area, so ask staff whether demos are available.

Local tip

Phone ahead through your hotel front desk or a Chinese-speaking friend to check that a specific model is in stock. New models can have long waits: at a different Shanghai DJI store, a Pocket 3 needed a pre-order with about a three-month wait in February 2025.

Watch out

(1) The 2026 evidence is DJI's own page only. No independent 2025 or 2026 report was found that the store is trading, so phone before you go. (2) Street numbers differ between sources: older sources give 222 (DJI's 2016 opening release, as summarised) or 232 (SmartShanghai's 2019 listing), while DJI's own page says 119 Madang Road, which Ctrip also prints as Xintiandi's own address. Show the driver 马当路119号 and ask for the DJI store once you reach Xintiandi. (3) Drone-flying rules in China were not researched: ask staff, and do not fly in the city without checking. (4) Warranty terms for a foreign buyer and accepted payment methods are unknown. (5) The walking time is an estimate from map data.

What was checked, and what was not

Read on 15 Nov 2026

Read on 15 Nov 2026: DJI's official English store page lists the store at No. 119 Madang Road, Mon to Sun 10:00 to 22:00, about 600 m2 over two storeys, with a 20 m2 flight area and a Swift Service Center, and no closure or relocation notice. Older independent sources agree there is a DJI flagship in Xintiandi: DJI's December 2016 opening release (seen only in summaries), SmartShanghai's 2019 listing, and two 2022 design write-ups (gooood, 30 Sep 2022; Hi设计, 23 Oct 2022). Xintiandi's own Ctrip page prints 119 Madang Road as its address, which fits.

Not checked

No independent 2025 or 2026 report that the store is trading: the 2026 evidence is DJI's own page, which is undated. DJI's Chinese dealer list could not be read, so the unit numbers, a longer Chinese store name, a third phone number and a second DJI store at Qiantan Taikoo Li in Pudong are left out. Prices, stock, payment methods and warranty terms for a foreign buyer were not found. No rating was read. Walking times are estimates.
