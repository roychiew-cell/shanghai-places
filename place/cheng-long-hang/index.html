Good to know

Order steamed or drunken hairy crab, or the snowflake crab shell made with picked meat and roe. Michelin says an a la carte menu is available and sets need advance booking.

What recent diners say

Recent diners praised crab and crab-roe dishes, including a table of eight in October. November complaints focused on service and value. The overall and taste scores stayed at 4.8 from July through October. Read on Dianping on 21 Nov 2026.

Planning your visit

Book seven seats or a private room in advance. Michelin has a booking link. Ask for the current crab set and price, and whether staff can pick the crab.

Local tip

The Michelin page explicitly names drunken crab and snowflake crab shell; steamed whole crab appears on the Qunar listing.

Watch out

Michelin live listing describes crab dishes but does not date a 2026 seasonal menu. Call to confirm the November menu, crab size, service charge and seating for seven.
