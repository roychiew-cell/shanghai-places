Why go

Amap listings read 28 Nov 2026 named Balabala (L3111-L3112), Anta Kids (L3086), moodytiger (L3093-L3095), DAVE&BELLA (L3105) and Teenie Weenie Kids on that floor. adidas Kids and FILA Kids were also listed, without a floor. Only the first results page was read, and listings can be out of date.

How to get there

Take Line 13 to 金沙江路, follow the signs for 环球港, then check the directory for L3. Start with Balabala, Anta Kids and DAVE&BELLA. Sizes are by height in cm; ask about exchanges before paying. See the size guide on the Kids' clothing page.

Local tip

TARANIS on L3 is listed for children's shoes. Amap's L3 listing named only 'JNBY' is not confirmed as the children's line.

Watch out

Only the first page of Amap results was read on 28 Nov 2026. Shops, floors and hours may have changed. Confirm the directory and stock on arrival; no current prices were found.
