Grand Gateway 66 (Hang Lung), kids' zone on 4F 港汇恒隆广场

Mall with a kids' zone on 4F, North Tower · Xuhui

CheckedHours not found

Head for 4F of the North Tower, a whole kids' zone with a reading corner. Shop lists change fast.

Address

No. 1 Hongqiao Road, Xuhui District (Hang Lung's own page). Kids' zone: 4F, North Tower (北座4楼).

Price
Expect mid to premium prices (a judgement, not a price check): the mall is luxury-led, and the kids' brands named include MINI PEACE, erdos KIDS and the French label Kenzo Kids. No price list found.
Metro
Xujiahui station (Lines 1, 9, 11); Hang Lung says the mall sits on top of it. Board Line 1 at 一大会址·黄陂南路 (Site of the First CPC National Congress · South Huangpi Road; Lines 1 and 14, a separate station from Xintiandi station, Lines 10 and 13), ride towards Xinzhuang as shown on the platform and get off at Xujiahui: 4 stops, no change.
Area
Xujiahui, Xuhui District

Why go

The best-organised single stop found: the North Tower has a whole kids' zone on 4F, refreshed in June 2026 with a reading corner of about 100 m2. Hang Lung (the mall's owner) ran June 2026 kids' events there with MINI PEACE and erdos KIDS, and a May 2026 report lists the French label Kenzo Kids as a new store in the mall (floor not stated). A November 2024 trade article names the Korean kids' label BLUEDOG (蓝狗), the kids' streetwear label YEK and Prima Rider kids' bikes on 4F North. The mall sits on Xujiahui station, the same station as Neo Young 6 (pick 4), so one trip can cover both.

How to get there

Take Line 1 (or a taxi) to 港汇恒隆广场 (虹桥路1号), then go up to 4F in the North Tower (北座4楼). Allow about 2 to 3 hours if you add Neo Young 6 (pick 4); that is a guess, not a timed visit. Sizes are by height in cm: measure the child first and buy that number or the next size up (size guide at the end of this section).

Local tip

A reading corner of about 100 m2 opened on 4F in June 2026, a place for children to sit while you shop.

Watch out

Shop lists change fast: a November 2024 trade article shows two 4F kids' shops replaced by new ones, so do not plan around one named shop. For anything for a child under 3, look for the A class (A类) label with the words 婴幼儿用品. A Shanghai regulator notice of about September 2024 (80 batches tested, 17 failed) found failing baby and children's garments in shops in several big malls, including a baby-trouser batch sold at Grand Gateway 66 itself, as well as online. The mall has an ongoing works plan (Hang Lung calls it an optimisation plan; a Kimpton hotel is due there in the second half of 2027), so some construction barriers are possible. Returns are decided shop by shop: keep receipts and tags and ask before paying. Opening hours were not found: confirm on arrival.
