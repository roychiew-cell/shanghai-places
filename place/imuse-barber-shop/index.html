IMUSE Barber Shop (Taian Road) IMUSE BARBER SHOP(泰安路)

Long-running independent men's barbershop · Xuhui

CheckedAddress and hours read · 15 Nov 2026

Two shops share this name, so go to the Xuhui one. It has cut men's hair since 2008 and opens daily.

Address

33 Taian Lu (Taian Road), Xuhui District. The shop's own Instagram and a Sep 2024 Sohu walk guide give 泰安路33号. Apple Maps shows only 'Taian Road Lane 27' and Amap shows only the district. Navigate to the map pin named 'IMUSE BARBER SHOP(泰安路)' rather than the door number.

Why go

Opened in 2008 by founder Henry (Tigerhood and China News Service, 2024) and still running: its own Instagram posted in spring 2026 and Amap reviews run to 7 Nov 2026. China News Service (May 2024) says the minimum haircut spend is CNY 280 and that the owner doubled the shop's floor area and kept prices. It names barbers Oscar (nearly 8 years at the shop as of May 2024) and Liu Qifeng (about 10 years in the trade). Amap reviewers in 2026 describe reliable technique, good aesthetics and a quiet haircut, but the quality is steady rather than exceptional (Dianping 4.2).

Independent or chain

Independent, two shops: 泰安路33号 (Xuhui, the one to use) and 中江路629号 (Putuo). Make sure the driver goes to 泰安路33号. The shop probably moved from Yongfu Lu to Taian Lu between 2020 and 2024 (inferred; no source states the move).

How to get there

Phones: 021-5403 1679 and 021-6403 1135 (both on Amap; the shop's Instagram gives 021-64031135 and Apple Maps gives 021-5403 1679). Ask your hotel front desk or a Chinese-speaking friend to call; no mini-program or online booking was found. Show the driver 泰安路33号 and the map pin named 'IMUSE BARBER SHOP(泰安路)' on Amap or Apple Maps. Card or wallet payment not verified.

Local tip

Tell the driver it is the Taian Road shop in Xuhui (泰安路33号). A 2024 Sohu walk guide lists haircut, shave and face shaving, so ask whether a shave is offered and what it costs. An Amap review (7 Nov 2026) describes a quiet haircut with coffee next door.

About the ratings

Amap 4.9 (12 ratings), read 15 Nov 2026. Dianping 4.2 (471 ratings), relayed on an Apple Maps place page read on 14 Nov 2026 (not read on Dianping itself).

Watch out

Two shops share the name: use 泰安路33号 in Xuhui, not 中江路629号 in Putuo. The door number is 泰安路33号 on the shop's own Instagram and a 2024 walk guide, but Apple Maps shows 'Taian Road Lane 27', so follow the map pin. The CNY 280 minimum (2024) does not match the CNY 200 to 217 average spends, so ask the price first. An 8 Oct 2026 Amap review says the front desk and a barber were curt to someone who only asked to use the toilet, so expect a shop that is not warm to non-customers. Photos are not allowed in the cutting area. English is not documented, and no 2026 source confirms the shave.
