Why go

Go here if you are already at the Pudong sights, not as a special trip for clothes. The mall's own kids page lists Balabala (巴拉巴拉, ages 0 to 14) and TeenieWeenie on 2F, with LEGO (乐高) and TARANIS (泰兰尼斯, kids' shoes) also on 2F and Toys R Us (玩具反斗城) on 6F. Bloomberg (18 Feb 2026) describes a Kids' Third Home zone with play areas, theatres and events, with a playground added in 2025.

How to get there

Take Line 14 direct to 陆家嘴 (Lujiazui) and use Exit 2, which leads straight into the mall (follow the 正大广场 signs). Kids' clothing is on 2F and toys on 6F according to the mall's page; check the floor directory on arrival. Pair it with other Pudong plans rather than making a special trip for clothes. Sizes are by height in cm: measure the child first and buy that number or the next size up (size guide at the end of this section).

Local tip

Stroller and umbrella rental is listed among the mall's member benefits; ask on arrival whether visitors can use it.

Watch out

Only two kids' clothing brands are listed on the mall's kids page, so the clothes choice is thinner than at picks 1 and 2. The shop list comes from the mall's own page (copyright 2025), and no 2025 or 2026 report was found naming these shops, so check the floor directory on arrival.
