Jade Buddha Temple (Yufo Chan Temple) 上海玉佛禅寺

Working Buddhist temple founded in 1882 · Putuo

CheckedEntry and hours read · 15 Nov 2026

Arrive by about 15:00 for the jade Buddha chamber, which may charge CNY 10. Gates free; shuts 16:30.

Address

170 Anyuan Road, Putuo District (enter from Anyuan Road)

Open in Amap (opens Amap, or its website, in a new tab)

See on the map

Drop a pin in Amap instead (opens Amap, or its website, in a new tab)

Hours
Daily 08:00-16:30; 07:00-16:30 on the 1st and 15th of each lunar month and major festivals. No reservation needed except named Spring Festival nights.
Tickets
Free general entry since 13 Jun 2023 (except Chinese New Year's Eve night and the first 15 days of the lunar new year). Older guides cite CNY 10 for the jade Buddha room: likely outdated; ask at the gate.
Time
About 1 to 1.5 hours (estimate).
Best time
Weekday mid-morning to early afternoon; it closes at 16:30, before sunset. Arrive by about 15:00 to see the jade Buddha chamber.
Metro
Line 13, Jiangning Road (江宁路), Exit 3, about a 5-minute walk along Jiangning Road. The 2026 notice also names Line 7 Changshou Road as walkable.
Area
Putuo District, near Jiangning Road

Why it matters

A living temple tied to modern Shanghai. The monk Huigen brought five jade Buddhas from Burma and left two in Shanghai; the temple began as an 1882 thatched hut and moved several times. In 1946 the reform monk Taixu based his effort to modernise Chinese Buddhism here, and he died here in 1947.

What to see

The axis of halls from the Heavenly Kings Hall to the Great Hall; the Jade Buddha chamber, with a seated jade Buddha and a small Burmese jade reclining Buddha; a 4 m white-marble reclining Buddha from Singapore.

How to get there

Metro Line 13 to Jiangning Road, Exit 3, then walk along Jiangning Road. Taxi: show 上海市普陀区安远路170号. Dress modestly and be quiet in the halls.

Local tip

Per the Spring Festival 2026 notice: vegetarian noodles are served on site; alcohol sprays and candles are not allowed.

Watch out

Protective repairs have run since July 2014, and a five-storey Buddhist museum and 13-storey research centre are being built on the north side until Dec 2029, so parts may be hoarded off; the temple says it stays open.

What was checked, and what was not

Read on 15 Nov 2026

The temple's notice (Jun 2023) and history page, read 15 Nov 2026, confirm free entry, hours and history; its news runs to April 2026. ChinaHighlights (24 Sep 2026) independently prints free admission and 08:00-16:30.

Not checked

Whether the jade Buddha chamber has a separate CNY 10 fee (older guides say yes; the temple and a Sep 2026 guide say general entry is free); photography rules inside the halls.
