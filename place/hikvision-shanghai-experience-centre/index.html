Official page read

The evidence for this one has a gap. Check on the day or call ahead.

Why go

Hikvision's own conference-panel site lists a Shanghai experience centre among its national centres. The site is about the WonderHub meeting panels, but it does not say what is on display, and it gives the address and a hotline only.

How to get there

No access rule is printed. The address is the fifth floor of Building B8, so it is an office floor and not a shop front. Ask your hotel front desk or a Chinese-speaking friend to phone 400-800-5998 (printed on the site) and ask whether visitors are admitted and whether a booking is needed.

Local tip

The MAXHUB experience point in this section is also in the Hongmei Road subdistrict of Xuhui; OpenStreetMap's two street points are about 1.3 km apart, so one taxi ride could link both if both agree to a visit.

Watch out

No opening hours, access rule or list of displayed products is given. The site's data file with the centre addresses was last modified on 21 May 2026 (server header), and the company text on the site gives figures to 31 Dec 2018. The centre may not be open to visitors without prior arrangement.
