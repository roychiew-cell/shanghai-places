Independent men's barbershop · Huangpu

Hours
Amap (read 15 Nov 2026): Tue to Fri 12:00 to 20:00, Sat and Sun 11:00 to 19:00, closed Monday. SmartShanghai (editor text Mar 2026): Mon to Fri 12:00 to 20:00, Sat and Sun 10:00 to 18:00. The sources disagree on Monday and weekend hours and the shop's own channels give none, so go Tuesday to Friday between 12:00 and 20:00, after a phone or WeChat check.
Price
No 2026 price list found. Amap average spend is CNY 273 (5 ratings, read 15 Nov 2026). Dec 2024 list prices on a SmartShanghai first-visit promo page: haircut plus beard trim CNY 398, haircut plus shave CNY 598. In Aug 2020 a cut alone cost CNY 198 (junior), 328 (senior) or 368 (master) (10100.com). Budget roughly CNY 300 to 600 for a cut plus shave (an estimate only) and ask the tier price before you sit.
Metro
South Shaanxi Road (Lines 1, 10, 12), about an 11-minute walk (SmartShanghai). South Shaanxi Road station is one stop from Xintiandi station on Line 10 (stop count not confirmed on a metro map).
Area
Huangpu (Former French Concession), Julu Lu near Shaanxi Nan Lu

Why go

Founded February 2015 by Anna Elliott (born in Scotland to American parents; cut hair in New York before moving to Shanghai). It is a men's barbershop for short hair, beard trims and a hot-towel straight-razor shave, and it is the best-evidenced of the shops checked. Barbers work in three tiers (junior, senior, master), so the price depends on who you book, and the shop's SmartShanghai text lists English and Chinese service. The quality claim rests on reputation and steady reviews, not awards: Time Out Shanghai (2017, when the shop was on Kangding Lu) called the cut high quality.

Independent or chain

Independent, one shop now. The older Kangding Lu shop closed when its lease ended and the shop moved to Julu Road (City News Service, Jun 2025; Jing'an District government site, Jan 2025). An earlier Pudong branch (Kerry Parkside, 花木路1388号) is marked closed on SmartShanghai.

How to get there

Three ways to book. (1) WeChat: search the mini-program 'DocsBarberShop' and pick a barber and a time slot (SmartShanghai); the screens are probably in Chinese. (2) Walk in: the shop's Instagram bio says walk-ins are welcome and City News Service (Jun 2025) says appointments are not necessary, but Saturday afternoons are busy. (3) Ask your hotel front desk or a Chinese-speaking friend to phone the shop (its number is on Amap, SmartShanghai and the shop's Instagram). Show the taxi driver: 巨鹿路360-7号 DOC'S BARBERSHOP(巨鹿店). SmartShanghai lists local and international cards (not tested at the till).

Local tip

Go on a weekday afternoon. The shop uses clipper guards (City News Service, Jul 2026), so naming a guard number such as 'number 2 on the sides' should work. Ask which barber tier you are booking, and the price for that tier, before you sit.

About the ratings

Amap 4.7 (5 ratings), read 15 Nov 2026.

Address notes

The number 360-7 is the same on Amap, SmartShanghai, City News Service and the shop's own Instagram and Facebook captions. SmartShanghai also carries a Jing'an district tag; Amap says Huangpu.

Watch out

Monday and weekend opening times conflict between Amap and SmartShanghai. Weekend afternoons are busy. One of the five Amap reviews shown (5 Sep 2026) calls the shop expensive and not good. Ignore any 'from about CNY 150' cut price you may see on LocalShanghai: that page still shows the shop's closed Kangding Lu address and copies older listing data. A similarly named Doc's Barbershop in Chengdu is not linked to this shop by any source read, so use the Julu Road address.

What was checked, and what was not

What was read

Read on 15 Nov 2026: the shop's own Instagram (@docsbarbershop) has posts dated 6, 11, 17 and 26 Oct 2026 and one of 9 Sep 2026 that names 360-7 Julu Road, and its phone number matches Amap and SmartShanghai. City News Service (14 Jul 2026) lists the shop at this address, open 12:00 to 20:00, operating for 11 years. The move from Kangding Road to Julu Road after the lease ended is reported by the Jing'an District government site (10 Jan 2025) and City News Service (16 Jun 2025). A SmartShanghai event page confirms a 10th-anniversary party at 360-7 Julu Lu on 22 and 23 Mar 2025, and SmartShanghai marks the Pudong branch closed.

Not checked

Opening hours (sources conflict; the shop's own channels give none). 2026 prices: no price list was found, and the Dec 2024 figures are expired promo list prices. English-language service comes from the shop's SmartShanghai text and was not tested. 'Walk-ins welcome' is the shop's own statement. Card and phone-wallet payment at the till. The Amap rating rests on only 5 ratings and was read once, and Dianping could not be read because its bot check blocked access. SmartShanghai's own page says 'last verified more than 6 months ago'.
