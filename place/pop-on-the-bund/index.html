Good to know

Its terrace looks across the Huangpu River to Lujiazui. The Michelin Guide describes contemporary European food, including grilled meat, seafood, foie gras pâté and black-truffle pasta.

Planning your visit

Book through Three on the Bund's restaurant reservation page or call the published restaurant number. Ask explicitly for a river-facing terrace or window table; availability and any terrace guarantee are not confirmed. Use the Three on the Bund entrance at 17 Guangdong Road and take the lift to floor 7; this entrance is reported by SmartShanghai and a Trip.com visitor, not confirmed on the venue site.

Local tip

For a river view, ask about terrace seating when booking. Michelin lists brunch and names bacon and eggs; confirm the current menu before ordering.

Watch out

Published restaurant hours conflict: SmartShanghai says restaurant daily 11am–11pm and bar 2pm–1am; Michelin says weekdays 11:30–14:00 and 17:00–22:00 (weekends 11:30–14:30 and 17:00–22:00); Dianping says 11:30–14:30 and 17:00–22:00, all read 21 Nov 2026. The open terrace may be weather-dependent in late November; winter use and any dress rule were not verified. The Michelin listing is Selected, not a star or Bib Gourmand.
