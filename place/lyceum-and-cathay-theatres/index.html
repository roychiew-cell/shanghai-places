Lyceum Theatre (1931) and Cathay Theatre (1932): the old-theatre corner west of Xintiandi 兰心大戏院（茂名南路57号）与国泰电影院（淮海中路870号）

Two surviving old theatres, both in use · Huangpu

LikelyOne 29 Nov show listed · 18 Nov 2026

Two theatres from 1931 and 1932 stand 325 m apart; see the lobbies. One Lyceum screening is listed for 29 Nov.

Address

In English

Lyceum Theatre, 57 Maoming South Road. Cathay Theatre, 870 Huaihai Middle Road, near Maoming Road. About 325 m apart.

Why it matters

Lyceum opened its present hall in February 1931 for the Amateur Dramatic Club and is a regular film-festival venue. Cathay opened on 1 January 1932 in Art Deco style and keeps its restored marble floor and film-reel door frames. Young film fans pick these old halls for the atmosphere.

What to see

Art Deco lobbies, mosaic staircases and film-reel details at Cathay; the 1931 hall at Lyceum. The stub-for-magnet swap (three stubs for a fridge magnet) is a Lyceum and Majestic custom, not Cathay's.

How to get there

Walk or taxi to 兰心大戏院 (Maoming South Road), then 325 m to 国泰电影院 on Huaihai Road. For films, ask for 原版 (original-language) sessions; buy at the box office.

Local tip

Arrive 30 minutes before the session to look at the lobbies, and keep your ticket stub: collecting stubs is a film-fan custom.

Watch out

Most Lyceum programmes in October 2026 were Chinese children's plays: choose a dance or music event, or just see the lobbies and facades. Chinese films carry Chinese subtitles only. Old listings (SmartShanghai, 2019) are stale.
