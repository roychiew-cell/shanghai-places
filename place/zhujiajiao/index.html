Zhujiajiao water town 朱家角古镇

Half-day trip inside Shanghai, by metro · Qingpu

CheckedTicket page read

This canal town is free; allow about 4 hours there plus about 3 hours of travel. Arrive about 09:30.

Address

In English

Official visitor centre: No. 555 Kezhiyuan Road, Zhujiajiao Town, Qingpu District, Shanghai. Metro: Zhujiajiao station (Line 17), Exit 1.

Hours
Streets open all day and free. Paid sights and boat sales daily 08:30-17:00 (official site). No weekly closing day is stated.
Tickets
Town free. Three-sight combo CNY 60 (Kezhi Garden, Hexin Garden 和心园, Helong Art Museum 鹤龙美术馆) is booked with a mobile number; foreigner purchase unconfirmed, ask the visitor centre (021-59240077).
Time
Half a day: about 4 hours in town (average stay 4.2 hours), plus about 3 hours of travel there and back.
Best time
Weekday morning: arrive about 09:30, before tour groups (about 10:30). Late November: leave by about 16:30; sunset is about 16:50 and shops close about 17:00.
Metro
Line 17 to Zhujiajiao (朱家角站), Exit 1; trains about every 10 min; last train back 22:33. Bus 1510 (CNY 1) or a 5-20 min walk to the old town.
Area
Qingpu District, west Shanghai, beside Dianshan Lake; about 47 km from central Shanghai

Why it matters

A real example of how Jiangnan trade towns grew: a small Song-Yuan market village that became a large town by 1612 through canal transport and commerce. Fangsheng Bridge, a five-arch stone bridge, was begun in 1571 by the monk Xingchao. The Daqing Post Office (1903) is the only surviving Qing post office in East China.

What to see

Fangsheng Bridge (放生桥, 1571, five arches, 70.8 m); North Street (北大街, busy, tourist prices); Kezhi Garden (课植园); Daqing Post Office (大清邮局, 35 Xihu Street); and a hand-rowed boat under the bridge.

How to get there

Metro only: Line 10 to Hongqiao Railway Station, Line 17 to Zhujiajiao, Exit 1, then bus 1510 or walk. No booking is needed to enter the town.

Local tip

Boats are priced per boat (CNY 200 for 15 min, 300 for 20 min, up to 6 people): agree route and price at the booth, not with touts.

Watch out

Busy at weekends and holidays: 100,500 visits over the three-day Mid-Autumn holiday of 25-27 Sep 2026 (about 29% from abroad). Last boats 17:00. North Street restaurants charge tourist prices. Mostly canals and shops: do not expect museum depth.

What was checked, and what was not

Read by 16 Nov 2026

The town's official ticket page was read (combo CNY 60, hours 08:30-17:00), and 100,500 visits over 25-27 Sep 2026 (Xinhua Finance, 3 Oct 2026) and over 10 million visits in 2025 were confirmed. The official ticket page is undated.

Not checked

Whether foreigners can buy the combo without a Chinese mobile number. The bridge (1571) and post-office (1903) dates have not been confirmed since.
