Second-hand book stalls at Fuyou Building B2 (check before going) 福佑商厦地下2层旧书摊（福民商厦4楼旧书市集摊主去向之一）

Possible Sunday stop · Huangpu

UnconfirmedA lead, not a plan · 19 Nov 2026

April reports: book stalls leaving the Fumin Building, some at B2 of the Fuyou Building. Check first.

Address

B2, Fuyou Building, 225 Fuyou Road, Huangpu, near the City God Temple; the stalls' former home was the 4th floor of the Fumin Building, 223 Fuyou Road.

Hours
Reported to trade on Sundays; current hours are unconfirmed. An April 2026 report describes the B2 stalls as busier in the afternoon.
Tickets
Free. Prices were reported low at the old market (many books from CNY 1); bargaining is normal and small cash is useful.
Time
1.5-2 hours.
Best time
Sunday afternoon at the B2 stalls (an April 2026 report says it is busier then, with more young visitors).
Metro
Line 10 or 14 Yuyuan Garden, Exit 7 (SmartShanghai), about 12 min on foot.
Area
Huangpu, north of Yuyuan Garden, Fuyou Road, near the City God Temple

A lead, not a plan

The evidence for this one is little, weak or old. Treat it as a lead, not a plan, and have a second choice ready.

Why it matters

A retiree-run Sunday market of second-hand books and paper ephemera: sellers from the old Wenmiao market (closed 2021) reopened on the Fumin Building's 4th floor in January 2026; April 2026 reports said most were due to leave by the end of April, some already at B2 of the Fuyou Building nearby; a report of 28 April found the 4th floor still open with fewer than ten book stalls. Locals trade old comics, magazines, maps, ration stamps and photographs, a paper archive of ordinary Shanghai.

What to see

Rows of wooden-board tables piled with Chinese books (some Russian, a few English), 1960s-90s lianhuanhua picture-story books, old maps (for example a 1967 Shanghai bus map), magazines, postcards, food ration stamps and photographs.

How to get there

Taxi to 福佑路225号 (福佑商厦, Fuyou Building) and go down to B2. The old site, 福佑路223号 (福民商厦, 4th floor), was due to close at the end of April 2026. The map pin marks the Fuyou Building as a whole; the B2 entrance is not verified.

Local tip

Go to the B2 market first; if it is shut, try Guji Bookstore's second-hand floor. Do not buy 'antiques' expecting authenticity; ask before photographing sellers.

Watch out

The market has been in flux. Xinmin Evening News (via Jiefang Daily, 12 Apr 2026) said most stallholders would leave the 4th floor of the Fumin Building by the end of April, for the Cao'an Road textile market, B2 of the Fuyou Building nearby (225 Fuyou Road) or Jubao City on Lingshi Road; Labor Daily (28 Apr 2026) found the 4th floor still open with fewer than ten book stalls, and sellers trading at Fuyou B2. No later report found, so whether either place trades this Sunday is unknown: ask your hotel front desk or a Chinese-speaking friend to check Amap or phone the day before.

Real and trading in early 2026: Yicai (23 Jan), Shanghai Observer (19 Jan), RADII (3 Mar) and SmartShanghai (23 Mar 2026) describe Sunday trading on the 4th floor of the Fumin Building. Dated reports: Labor Daily, 2 Apr 2026, and Xinmin Evening News via Jiefang Daily, 12 Apr 2026 (read 18 Nov 2026): most stallholders were due to leave the 4th floor by the end of April, for Cao'an Road, B2 of the Fuyou Building or Jubao City on Lingshi Road. Latest: Labor Daily, 28 Apr 2026 (read 19 Nov 2026): the 4th-floor market was staying open for now with fewer than ten book stalls left, and some sellers were trading at B2 of the Fuyou Building nearby.

Not checked

Whether the B2 stalls trade on Sundays in late November: no report after 28 Apr 2026 was found. Closing time and stall payment methods are unknown.
